# Work Contract 069 — Helpdesk And Support Administration

**Program:** WC-064 — Founder Commercial Governance Program Design
**Iteration:** 5
**Status:** DEFERRED OUTCOME CANDIDATE — GROOMING AND IMPLEMENTATION UNAUTHORIZED
**Depends on:** Real customer cases and stable boundaries from WC-065 through WC-068

## Outcome

If operational evidence proves a separate support capability necessary, define support
administration without absorbing commercial, employment, privacy, constitutional, provider, or
reconciliation decisions owned elsewhere.

## Objective And Success Measures

If activation evidence proves a separate capability necessary, deliver a support coordination
surface that improves acknowledgement, ownership, communication, escalation, and closure while
leaving every substantive decision with its constitutional owner. Success means a customer or
steward can follow a case from intake to evidenced resolution without support staff gaining a
financial, privacy, employment, provider, agent-lifecycle, or constitutional override.

## Evidence Required Before Grooming

- sufficient real case volume to define repeatable categories and service expectations;
- evidence distinguishing support requests from financial exceptions, employment corrections,
  privacy grievances, constitutional reviews, provider incidents, and agent-learning signals;
- named owner, escalation, response, retention, access, and customer-transparency obligations;
- a defensible support cost and staffing model; and
- proof that existing relationship, exception, and evidence capabilities cannot coherently own
  the required behavior.

## Candidate Capability Boundary

Case intake, classification, assignment, status, communication, evidence references, escalation,
resolution, and service review may be considered later. Helpdesk must never become an override
path around WBE, CE, customer rights, agent lifecycle governance, or Founder assurance.

## Deferral Rule

WC-069 remains deferred until the Product Owner demonstrates that real evidence satisfies every
grooming prerequisite. Anticipated support needs or generic administration patterns are not
sufficient authority to begin design or implementation.

## First Grooming Trigger And Evidence Sample

The Product Owner must provide an independently reviewed case study set derived from real support
demand. It must include frequency, channel, customer impact, current owner, handoffs, response and
resolution time, reopenings, unresolved cases, privacy classification, and cost. Cases must be
classified against existing WC-065 offerability, WC-066 employment oversight, WC-067 exception,
WC-068 proposal, grievance, privacy, security-incident, and constitutional-review paths.

Grooming may begin only if the evidence shows a repeatable coordination gap that cannot be closed
coherently by extending an existing owner capability.

## Decisions To Close During First Grooming

1. Decide build, extend-existing-capability, procure, or reject, with evidence and total-cost
   implications; “helpdesk is conventional” is not a valid basis.
2. Define case taxonomy, owner routing, priority, SLA/OLA, acknowledgement, escalation, reopening,
   closure, and customer communication without granting substantive decision authority.
3. Define channel and identity requirements, customer/participant visibility, accessibility,
   language, consent, attachments, and evidence-link behavior.
4. Define privacy classification, minimisation, retention, legal hold, erasure boundaries, search,
   export, and staff access segregation.
5. Define integration boundaries for WBE, employment, provider, security, privacy, grievance,
   agent-learning, and constitutional cases as references and handoffs, not copied truth.
6. Define service-quality and support-cost review feeding WC-068 without exposing individual
   customers to punitive profiling.

## Grooming Deliverables

- Evidence-backed capability decision and support operating model.
- Product case journeys, communication standards, accessibility outcomes, and acceptance matrix.
- Business taxonomy, ownership, SLA/OLA, escalation, staffing, and cost model.
- Enterprise/Solution build-or-integrate design with owner handoff and unavailable behavior.
- Data contract for case identity, timeline, messages, attachments, classification, retention,
  evidence references, search/export, and migration/no-migration decision.
- Security/Constitutional contract for assurance, role segregation, privacy, abuse, customer
  rights, immutable evidence, and absolute non-override controls.

## Required Acceptance Scenarios

| Scenario | Required result |
|---|---|
| Ordinary information/support request | Correct acknowledgement, owner, status, communication, and evidenced closure |
| Billing or reconciliation dispute | Routed to WBE-owned process; support cannot issue or fabricate adjustment |
| Employment correction or agent-quality concern | Routed to WC-066/lifecycle owner without direct mutation |
| Privacy grievance, security incident, or constitutional concern | Immediate protected routing with access and disclosure controls |
| Misclassification or owner rejection | Auditable reroute/reopen without losing history or silently closing the case |
| Owner unavailable or SLA breach | Explicit escalation and customer-visible unresolved status |
| Attachment, search, or export request | Minimised, authorized, retained/erased under approved policy, and tenant isolated |
| Override attempt or evidence failure | Deny/fail closed; support cannot bypass an owner or claim resolution |
