## 1. Objective

Define one sustainable, resilient and constitutionally governed employment interface that every
WAOOAW Digital Professional must support without erasing professional specialization.

The interface has three universal conversational phases:

1. **Onboard, Induct and Groom**;
2. **Goal, Plan, Milestone and Calendar Formation**; and
3. **Operations, Billing and Performance Management**.

Every phase must pair one durable professional conversation with an authoritative visual reflection
of current progress, completed work, in-progress work, pending work, why each pending item is
required, its accountable owner, the capability or milestone it blocks, evidence/freshness and the
next available action.

The platform defines the common employment semantics and gates. Each agent defines the domain
expertise, vocabulary, dependency types, planning method, work types and outcome interpretation that
conform to those semantics. DMA is the first proof case; Trading and Private Tutor provide
universality challenges.

  other Solution/Data/AI/Runtime office decisions;
- changes to `src/`, `web/`, tests, workflows, deployment, cloud resources, providers, DNS, secrets,
  customer traffic or production state;
- activation of a new Agent Base Specification or Platform-Agent Contract version before all
  affected agents have passing conformance evidence;
- claims that any current agent is customer-ready solely because its type-level activation gate
  passed;
- self-approval, self-merge or invocation of institutional review not requested by the Founder.

### 2.3 Non-negotiable constitutional boundaries

1. Conversation is the primary customer authoring path; forms may supplement but not replace it.
2. Browser state and chat history are not authoritative plan, readiness, billing or performance
   truth.
3. Capability and trust never grant authority.
4. Business outcomes lead every customer performance view; technical metrics remain supporting
   evidence.
5. WBE remains authoritative for billing, allowance, forecast and commercial consequence.
6. CE remains authoritative for constitutional validation and constitutional evidence.
7. Emergency Stop remains reachable in every phase and is never gated by readiness or billing.
8. Unknown, stale, partial, disputed, unavailable and blocked remain distinct from success.
9. A mandatory protocol version is not activated until every offered agent has passing evidence for
   that exact version.
10. Implementation requires a later bounded Work Contract and explicit current-session Founder
    authorization.
