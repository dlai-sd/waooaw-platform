# Work Contract 068 — Portfolio Economics And Institutional Learning

**Program:** WC-064 — Founder Commercial Governance Program Design
**Iteration:** 4
**Status:** PLANNED OUTCOME CANDIDATE — DETAILED GROOMING AND IMPLEMENTATION UNAUTHORIZED
**Depends on:** Settled multi-cohort evidence from WC-065 through WC-067

## Outcome

Enable Founder review of WAOOAW as a portfolio of constitutional professions, using evidence to
improve commercial policy, offering composition, provider envelopes, and governed learning while
preserving each owner's Decision Space.

## Objective And Success Measures

Deliver one portfolio review that converts settled multi-cohort evidence into governed proposals,
not automatic optimization. Success requires the Founder to compare professions, offerings,
versions, providers, cohorts, and policy outcomes using consistent denominators and confidence;
identify concentration, resilience, value, and margin patterns; and route each proposal to the
owner that may decide it.

## Candidate Capability Boundary

- institution, platform, agent type/version, offering/plan, provider/cost ingredient, customer,
  employment, cohort, margin, and reconciliation-exception review levels;
- contribution and fully loaded planning economics without presenting planning allocations as
  settled WBE truth;
- provider concentration, resilience, cost-change, and offering exposure patterns;
- repeated goal/resource mismatch and agent-version performance patterns;
- proposals to create, revise, suspend, or retire offerings;
- governed commercial-policy, provider-envelope, customer-goal, and agent-learning proposals; and
- review of calculated-risk outcomes against the Founder-approved umbrella policy.

## Excluded

Automated constitutional amendments, direct agent modification, unsupported causal claims,
customer-level punitive profiling, and optimization based on incomplete or provisional evidence.

## Grooming Rule

No detailed forecasting or portfolio optimization is specified until sufficient settled cohort
evidence exists to support the proposed precision.

## First Grooming Trigger And Evidence Sufficiency

The Product Owner, Data Architect, WBE owner, and Constitutional Analyst must jointly attest that
the available evidence includes multiple settled cohorts, documented WC-065 policy decisions,
WC-066 employment outcomes, and WC-067 resolved exceptions. The sufficiency record must state
coverage, missingness, survivorship/selection risks, comparability limits, and prohibited claims.
No fixed cohort count or statistical confidence threshold is invented by this Work Contract.

## Decisions To Close During First Grooming

1. Define valid portfolio units, cohort definitions, comparison periods, denominators, allocation
  semantics, and minimum sufficiency by owner-approved policy.
2. Distinguish settled WBE economics, management planning allocations, expected projections, and
  non-financial customer/professional outcomes.
3. Define concentration, resilience, cost-change, calculated-risk, and outcome-pattern review
  without implying causation unsupported by the evidence.
4. Define proposal routes for offering creation/revision/suspension/retirement, provider envelope,
  commercial policy, customer-goal guidance, and agent learning.
5. Define anti-discrimination, privacy, small-cohort suppression, customer-level drill-down, and
  non-punitive use controls.
6. Define how accepted/rejected proposals and later outcomes feed policy learning.

## Grooming Deliverables

- Evidence-sufficiency and comparability record with exclusions and uncertainty.
- Business portfolio capability and value model; Product review questions and decision outcomes.
- WBE/Data semantic contract for settled measures, planning allocations, cohorts, dimensions,
  lineage, retention, suppression, and effective dating, plus migration/no-migration decision.
- Enterprise/Solution design for owner-sourced portfolio projections, proposal routing, policy
  versioning, evidence, resilience, and owner unavailability.
- Security/Constitutional contract for minimisation, anti-profiling, small-cohort protection,
  Decision Space, agent-lifecycle governance, and prohibited automated action.
- Independently reviewed acceptance matrix and implementation traceability package.

## Required Acceptance Scenarios

| Scenario | Required result |
|---|---|
| Comparable settled cohorts | Founder can compare value/economics with denominator, period, lineage, and confidence visible |
| Mixed settled and provisional inputs | Provisional inputs are excluded or visibly separated; no blended settled claim |
| Provider concentration or resilience risk | Evidence-backed proposal routes to the provider/commercial owner; no direct switch |
| Repeated calculated-risk success or failure | Policy proposal references original decisions, exposure, and settled outcomes |
| Offering or agent-version pattern | Proposal routes to offering or lifecycle governance without direct modification |
| Small, biased, or incomplete cohort | Suppress, qualify, or return insufficient evidence; no punitive profiling |
| Owner rejects proposal | Decision and reason remain evidenced; no automatic override |
| Cross-tenant, stale, or evidence-failure path | Deny/fail closed without leaking customer-level information |
