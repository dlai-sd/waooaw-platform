## Objective

Make Goal Orchestrator Operating Model vNext faster and cheaper without reducing effectiveness,
correctness, completeness, owner accountability, independent review, or Founder control. Future
sessions must apply these controls automatically from ratified repository instructions rather
than depend on a custom prompt.

## Required Outcomes

1. A Contribution Necessity Gate distinguishes reusable approved evidence from bounded
   continuation and genuinely new material contribution.
2. Reused evidence passes an explicit applicability, version, hash, scope, assumption, and
   staleness test.
3. M0/M1 classifications pass an adversarial materiality challenge and remain auditable.
4. Every Work Component has a machine-checkable completeness ledger; cost exhaustion cannot
   become completion evidence.
5. Material changes produce direct and indirect dependency-impact records before delta review.
6. Model selection follows materiality and complexity, with deterministic validation and
   escalation rather than default frontier use or unsafe cheap-model use.
7. Every Execution Plan declares context, token/cost, and handoff budgets with stop-and-
   consolidate and replan semantics.
8. Initial baselines receive complete protected review; later delta review is permitted only
   against approved, hash-pinned baselines and validated dependency impact.
9. Goal Orchestrator sessions load the durable vNext standard automatically during BOOTSTRAP.
10. The operating model preserves all R-095 safeguards, GOA/Acceptance chronology, Decision
    Spaces, G-13 separation, append-only evidence, independent M3 gates, and no self-review.

## Quality Invariants

- Optimize contexts and repeated reasoning, never required decisions, evidence, or gates.
- Uncertainty classifies upward.
- No owner is skipped solely to meet cost.
- Evidence reuse is an evidence-backed decision, not filename matching or absence of change.
- Missing, conflicting, stale, or inapplicable evidence remains explicit and blocks dependent
  completion.
- Cost thresholds stop and replan work; they never approve partial work.
- Cheap-model output cannot decide protected or ambiguous work without required escalation.
- Initial baselines receive complete review; delta review requires an approved baseline.
- Primary-executor continuity does not create authority to decide or review another Decision Space.
- No source, migration, test, generated client, build artifact, provider activation, deployment,
  production change, implementation authorization, PR approval, or merge is included.
