## Outcome

Define one Founder Commercial Governance program across WC-065 through WC-069 before any
iteration enters implementation. The design must let WAOOAW answer:

> Can WAOOAW responsibly offer this approved constitutional professional, with these skills and
> customer goals, at this price and resource envelope, while preserving customer outcomes,
> transparency, margin, and constitutional obligations?

The Founder View is an institutional decision system. It is not a generic administration
dashboard, a token-cost dashboard, or a new source of billing or financial truth.

## Founder Policy Direction

- WAOOAW acts autonomously under an approved umbrella policy rather than routing every granular
  decision to the Founder.
- When goal or resource adjustments preserve the approved margin band and constitutional floors,
  WAOOAW may take a calculated, evidence-backed, documented risk within explicit limits.
- The Founder reviews patterns, exceptions, policy performance, and consequential proposals.
- Cost changes apply prospectively at renewal with notice and customer choice.
- Reconciliation uncertainty remains visibly provisional until WBE settles it.
- WAOOAW-managed Meta and Google advertising accounts are the MVP model; customer-owned account
  support is deferred.

The acceptable margin bands, risk limits, escalation thresholds, and minimum evidence required
for a defensible envelope remain owner-routed design decisions. This contract does not invent them.

## Stable Design Spine

The integrated design must fix the decisions that would be expensive or unsafe to reinterpret
between iterations:

1. WBE remains authoritative for pricing, wallet, usage, payment, tax, collection, refunds,
   credits, cost attribution, and reconciliation truth.
2. BP owns public Founder and customer orchestration; PR owns professional execution; AIR owns AI
   provider selection and execution; CTG owns authorized provider calls; CE owns constitutional
   authorization and immutable evidence.
3. Founder View may hold policy versions, proposals, decisions, projections, and evidence
   references, but never duplicate an owner's operational or financial truth.
4. Expected, provisional, and settled cost; customer price; billed and earned revenue; markup;
   contribution and fully loaded planning margin; tax; cash; receivables; refunds; and credits
   remain distinct.
5. Cost ingredients are governed configuration supporting direct, variable, fixed, shared,
   pass-through, usage-based, contingent, tax, commitment, and Founder-defined categories plus
   charging units beyond tokens.
6. Every consequential change has fresh assurance, impact preview, explicit confirmation,
   idempotency, version/conflict behavior, effective dating, immutable history, and
   evidence-before-success.
7. Customer-impacting changes preserve notice, review, choice, and grandfathering.
8. Agent learning is proposed through lifecycle governance; Founder View never rewrites skills,
   prompts, Decision Space, or agent versions directly.
9. Unknown, stale, unavailable, disputed, provisional, and settled states remain explicit.
10. Helpdesk remains outside the commercial-governance core until operational evidence justifies
    WC-069 detailed grooming.

### Implementation Detail Boundary

“Implementation detail” in these Work Contracts means a bounded work package stating behavior,
owner, dependencies, failure behavior, evidence, and verification. It does not mean INST-013 may
invent endpoint paths, database columns, event schemas, class names, numeric policy thresholds, or
screen components. Those details must trace to the approved Product, Business, Solution, Data,
Security, Enterprise, and Constitutional contributions produced during grooming.

Every activated implementation Work Contract must contain:

- a task-to-owner-contract traceability table;
- authoritative read and command paths with fail-closed behavior;
- an explicit migration or no-migration decision;
- generated-contract and backward-compatibility consequences;
- unit, contract, integration, constitutional, security, data, accessibility, and browser checks
  proportional to the affected surface;
- minimum affected-surface coverage of 90 percent under C-076; and
- independent implementation review separated from execution under C-065.
