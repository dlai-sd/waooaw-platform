## 1. Authority And Scope

This Work Contract defines the reusable architecture and ordered delivery plan for the customer
lifecycle that begins after a valid trial start or paid hire. It joins already accepted admission,
employment, billing, customer-workspace and runtime contracts without creating another deployable
service or replacing their authoritative state.

The Founder approved this architecture and ordered work plan on 2026-09-15 and authorized the
Platform IT Expert to implement the repository-local scope, run the Section 19.8 qualification only
in Docker and create an unmerged PR from `ib/095/agent-employment-lifecycle`. The direct Founder
assignment is the issue authority for this delivery. Implementation is limited to existing component
ownership in Sections 12 and 18 and does not authorize a new service, provider activation, cloud
mutation, deployment, customer traffic, version promotion, PR approval or merge.

The implementing professional must stop rather than invent any missing commercial policy,
constitutional rule, data retention, security assurance, lifecycle transition, adapter behavior or
customer-visible meaning. Razorpay merchant account details remain later configurable inputs: their
absence blocks WC095-05B provider binding and positive provider-backed acceptance, but does not block
WC095-05A, the Demo zero-price path or the remaining repository-local implementation.

## 2. Detailed Objective

Create one platform-owned lifecycle framework through which any admitted WAOOAW professional can be
trialled or hired, personalized, inducted into a customer's domain, configured with skills and goals,
operated, monitored, reviewed, changed, paused, stopped and ended under one constitutional and
commercial truth.

Prove that framework with DMA Release 1 so a customer can:

1. select DMA from an offerable catalogue and choose an eligible trial or hire path;
2. review an itemized Razorpay-backed checkout experience supporting the current standard method
  families enabled by Razorpay for the WAOOAW merchant account, including credit card, debit card,
  UPI, netbanking and wallets, without WAOOAW collecting payment credentials;
3. complete explicit contract and payment semantics, including an environment-governed Demo
  100-percent discount that overrides the payable amount, preserves the payment-gateway look and
  feel, and creates a truthful zero-price commercial record without browser or adapter bypass;
4. receive one immutable, relationship-bound `agentInstanceId` pinned to the admitted Release 1
   artifact and exact compatible contract stack;
5. personalize presentation without changing professional behavior or authority;
6. complete an agent-led induction that learns, classifies and confirms the customer's business
   context without turning inference into fact;
7. select admitted Skills, agree measurable goals, Decision Space, budget, review cadence and stop
   conditions, and explicitly verify the resulting operational mandate;
8. enter Operations only after server-owned eligibility is satisfied;
9. observe work, limitations, evidence, usage, agent performance and external business outcomes in
   the selected-agent pane of the Customer Portal; and
10. amend, reassess, pause, stop, resume, convert, suspend, terminate or migrate without losing
   identity, history, isolation, billing truth or constitutional evidence.

The result must make a second professional cheaper to introduce because that professional supplies
only admitted domain extensions: induction questions, vocabulary, Skill definitions, goal schemas,
outcome semantics, performance measures and adapter behavior. Identity, consent, payment,
relationship state, evidence, Stop, version pinning, lifecycle progression and portal grammar remain
universal platform responsibilities.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Architecture Principles

1. **One employment aggregate.** Business Platform owns the Employment Relationship and its
   customer-facing lifecycle. Readiness stages are projections/substates, not a parallel aggregate.
2. **One immutable customer instance.** Trial and paid continuation preserve the same relationship and
   `agentInstanceId`; paid authority is new and cannot be inherited from trial authority.
3. **Admission precedes offerability.** Only an ACTIVE, compatible, environment-offerable professional
   version may be selected for a new relationship.
4. **Configuration is versioned authority input.** Presentation preferences, confirmed context,
   Skill bindings, goals, Decision Space, budget and review policy remain separately typed and owned.
5. **Operations is derived server truth.** Payment, browser progress, adapter readiness, model
   confidence or a successful conversation cannot independently unlock work.
6. **Domain extension, not domain fork.** An agent supplies typed schemas and professional behavior;
   it cannot implement private hire, payment, activation, evidence, lifecycle or portal semantics.
7. **Evidence before success.** Governed transitions and customer-visible consequential success require
   authoritative owner confirmation and committed constitutional evidence.
8. **No silent change.** Every material change creates a new revision, relocks dependent operation and
   requires the prescribed reassessment or approval. History is never overwritten.
9. **Channel neutrality.** Web, WhatsApp and future channels share the same relationship and commands;
   channel state never becomes authority.
10. **Fail closed, disclose honestly.** Missing, stale, incompatible, uncertain or unavailable inputs
    block dependent work and produce a truthful customer-safe explanation.

## 6. Canonical Lifecycle Model

### 6.1 Authoritative Employment Relationship States

WC-095 reuses the accepted BP relationship lifecycle. It does not add `INDUCTED`, `CONFIGURED` or
`OPERATING` as new top-level relationship states. The implementation must resolve exact existing enum
names before source work; if accepted contracts disagree, architecture reopens rather than choosing.

Conceptually:

```text
DISCOVERED / EVALUATING
  -> TRIAL
  -> CONTRACT_ACCEPTED
  -> ACTIVATION_PENDING
  -> ACTIVE
  -> PAUSED | SUSPENDED | TERMINATED
```

Trial expiry, payment state and adapter health are projections owned by their source domains. They do
not become relationship states or substitute for them.

### 6.2 Agent Employment Lifecycle Profile

For one relationship and `agentInstanceId`, BP composes these universal readiness stages:

```text
ONBOARD -> INDUCT -> GOAL_VERIFICATION -> BUSINESS_OUTCOMES -> OPERATIONS
```

Each stage has `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `READY_FOR_CONFIRMATION`, `VERIFIED`, `BLOCKED`,
`REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED` or `UNAVAILABLE` as applicable. These states describe readiness only. Each
stage records its exact input revisions, output revision, owner, evidence state, freshness,
completion criteria, blocker reasons and next authorized action.

| Stage | Universal platform contract | Agent-specific extension | Completion gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboard | Presentation and accessibility preferences only | Default professional display/persona choices within admitted bounds | Customer accepts valid preferences; no authority or behavior changed. |
| Induct | Conversational context proposal, source classification, confirmation and correction | Domain vocabulary, minimum viable context schema, questions and validation | Required context fields are explicitly confirmed at a current revision; unknowns/limitations remain named. |
| Goal Verification | Skill selection, goal revisions, measures, baselines, cadence, Decision Space, budget and stop conditions | Admitted Skill/goal schemas and professional recommendations | Every operational Skill has a current verified goal or admitted exemption and a current authority snapshot. |
| Business Outcomes | Outcome definitions, evidence, attribution, review windows and limitations | Domain measures and interpretations | Required initial plan/outcome contract is customer-visible, evidence-bound and approved where consequential. |
| Operations | Eligibility, work, review, quality, usage, attention, pause/Stop and reassessment | Typed domain plans/results through the admitted adapter | BP confirms all dependencies current and eligible; PR receives one exact operational mandate. |

The profile may move forward incrementally and may relock a dependent stage. It never destroys a
prior verified revision. A change to Onboard presentation alone does not relock Operations. A material
change to confirmed context, selected Skill, goal, Decision Space, budget, contract, professional
binding or review policy relocks only affected work and outcomes until reassessed.

### 6.3 Operational Mandate Snapshot

Before PR may invoke domain work, BP creates or resolves an immutable mandate snapshot containing:

- tenant, relationship, `agentInstanceId` and actor/role bindings;
- relationship lifecycle and trial/live mode;
- exact professional, specification, admission, artifact, base-spec, DNA, PAC and adapter coordinates;
- selected Skill/version and compatible input/output schema digests;
- confirmed context, configuration, goal and Decision Space revisions;
- budget/allowance owner reference, review policy, approvals and Stop state;
- operational purpose, permitted actions, exclusions, deadline and idempotency identity; and
- CE decision/evidence context and WBE reservation/attribution context where applicable.

PR rejects a missing, stale, unsupported, mismatched, stopped or superseded mandate before domain
payload parsing. The DMA image cannot create, widen, select or persist this mandate.

## 7. Hire, Trial, Razorpay Payment And Demo Discount Contract

### 7.1 Shared Commercial Entry

Trial and hire remain distinct customer choices with the same constitutional rights. Trial is
explicit, bounded and non-consequential. Hire requires the exact accepted Employment Contract,
fresh authorized customer intent and an activation-eligible WBE outcome. Neither path may be selected
by a default checkbox, agent persuasion, browser inference or adapter response.

### 7.2 Razorpay Gateway Boundary And Deferred Account Readiness

Razorpay is the accepted payment gateway under ADR-022. WAOOAW uses Razorpay-hosted Checkout or the
accepted Razorpay hosted payment/subscription page for collection. WAOOAW must not render its own
card-number, CVV, bank credential, UPI PIN or wallet-authentication fields and must never receive,
store, log, proxy or replay those secrets.

The payment API and Customer Portal UI are delivered before live merchant onboarding is complete.
Positive-payable execution remains `PAYMENT_PROVIDER_CONFIGURATION_PENDING` until the Founder or
authorized commercial operator later supplies and approves the WAOOAW Razorpay account details. This
deferred input includes, as applicable:

- Razorpay merchant/account identity and mode (`TEST` or `LIVE`);
- checkout key ID, server-only key secret and webhook signing secret;
- approved callback/webhook origins and public checkout origin;
- legal merchant display name, logo/theme/contact details and customer-support disclosures;
- accepted plan/product/order mappings, settlement currency and enabled payment methods;
- subscription/mandate configuration, GST identity and invoice configuration; and
- Key Vault/GitHub environment secret references, rotation owner, effective time and readiness
  evidence.

No secret value belongs in this Work Contract, source, browser configuration, logs, test fixture or
constitutional evidence. The public checkout key/configuration may be returned only in the bounded
Razorpay initialization response after server-side readiness and origin validation. Key secret and
webhook secret remain server-side. Account details supplied later do not authorize deployment, live
payment, Production traffic or customer charging; each still requires its own current authority.

Provider readiness is one of:

| State | Meaning | Customer/API behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `NOT_CONFIGURED` | Required WAOOAW Razorpay account inputs are absent | Positive payable checkout is unavailable; no provider call or fake order. |
| `CONFIGURED_UNVERIFIED` | Inputs exist but ownership, mode, webhook or order round-trip is unproven | Positive payable checkout remains blocked and customer sees a retry-safe unavailable state. |
| `READY_TEST` | Test account and callback/webhook evidence pass | Test-mode positive payable journeys may run only in an authorized non-live environment. |
| `READY_LIVE` | Live merchant identity, credentials, webhook, plan/order and settlement checks pass | Live checkout is eligible only in an independently authorized environment. |
| `DEGRADED` | Previously ready gateway is unavailable or inconsistent | New positive payable sessions stop; existing uncertain outcomes reconcile before retry. |
| `REVOKED` | Credentials/account/mode were revoked or failed security policy | All new provider operations stop until separately restored and reverified. |

Demo 100-percent-discount completion does not require Razorpay account readiness because it creates
no provider order or payment attempt. It still requires valid WBE promotion, commercial and
activation evidence.

### 7.3 Razorpay Checkout Interface And Payment Methods

The Customer Portal owns the WAOOAW checkout composition; Razorpay owns payment credential entry,
method availability, authentication and payment processing. For a positive payable amount, the UI
launches the official Razorpay-hosted interface using only a short-lived, server-created checkout
session bound to the exact relationship, accepted contract, commercial quote, amount, currency,
environment and idempotency intent.

The interface must support the latest standard payment-method families that the WAOOAW Razorpay
merchant account, currency, customer device and Razorpay runtime declare eligible. The baseline
customer-visible families are:

| Method family | Required experience | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Credit card | Razorpay-hosted card selection, entry, authentication and result | WAOOAW never receives PAN, expiry, CVV, OTP or tokenized credential. |
| Debit card | Razorpay-hosted debit-card selection, entry and bank authentication | Same non-collection boundary as credit card. |
| UPI | Razorpay-provided UPI intent, collect, QR or mandate option when currently eligible | WAOOAW never asks for or receives a UPI PIN; availability is provider-declared. |
| Netbanking | Razorpay-hosted supported-bank selection and bank redirect/authentication | WAOOAW does not copy a bank list or handle bank credentials. |
| Wallet | Razorpay-hosted supported-wallet selection and wallet authentication | Wallet brands and availability come from Razorpay, not a hard-coded WAOOAW list. |
| Other future Razorpay method | Display only when returned as eligible by the accepted gateway contract | Adding an optional provider method cannot weaken pricing, consent, evidence or security gates. |

"Latest" means the payment methods and checkout capabilities currently enabled and returned by
Razorpay for the configured WAOOAW account. WAOOAW must not hard-code a claim that every method is
always available, clone Razorpay's credential UI, or silently enable a new financial consequence.
Provider-managed presentation may evolve compatibly; new recurring mandate, deferred-credit or other
material payment semantics require owner review before WAOOAW exposes them.

The WAOOAW payment step always shows, before launch or discount completion:

- professional, bundle and accepted Employment Contract version;
- subscription and wallet-seed line items, base amount, discount, taxable value, GST and amount due;
- billing frequency, renewal amount/date or truthful pending value, cancellation/refund consequence
  and whether recurring authorization will be requested;
- gateway name, environment/test indicator where applicable and supported payment-method families;
- promotion identity/description without secret eligibility rules; and
- one explicit `Pay securely with Razorpay` or `Complete fully discounted activation` command.

The page must provide loading, launch, customer-cancelled, failed, pending, captured, fully discounted,
configuration-pending and reconciliation states. Closing Checkout is not payment failure or consent
withdrawal. Browser callback success is never authoritative; only signature-verified WBE/Razorpay
reconciliation can make payment activation-eligible.

### 7.4 Payment API Contract

Implementation must amend the canonical BP/WBE contracts before UI code. It must not invent a second
public billing facade. The existing contract-linked onboarding-order operation returns a
discriminated result:

| Result kind | Required fields and behavior |
|---|---|
| `RAZORPAY_CHECKOUT_REQUIRED` | Opaque checkout-session ID, Razorpay order/subscription reference where applicable, public checkout key/config, exact amount/currency, merchant display metadata, enabled method families, expiry and reconciliation target; no secret. |
| `FULLY_DISCOUNTED` | Exact WBE quote/promotion versions, list price, discount, tax/payable values, renewal consequence, activation-eligible zero-price outcome reference and evidence state; no Razorpay order/config. |
| `PROVIDER_CONFIGURATION_PENDING` | Stable reason code, accountable owner, retryability and customer-safe next action; no leaked account/secret/readiness detail. |
| `COMMERCIAL_CONFLICT` | Changed contract, quote, promotion, amount, currency, eligibility or request hash; performs no provider or activation mutation. |
| `OUTCOME_UNRESOLVED` | Existing order/payment/zero-price intent requires owner reconciliation; retries join it and never create another order or charge. |

The public operation family must provide: current checkout projection; create/replay checkout intent;
read/reconcile intent status; and start/join activation after an activation-eligible WBE outcome. The
private WBE/Razorpay boundary must provide: provider readiness; create hosted order/session; verify
callback/webhook signature; reconcile provider status; cancel/expire session where supported; and
project payment/promotion outcome. Concrete paths and schemas are OpenAPI-first and generated-client
controlled.

Every intent binds actor, tenant, relationship, `agentInstanceId`, accepted contract version/hash,
commercial quote version/hash, amount, currency, promotion revision, environment, provider mode,
idempotency key and material request hash. Same binding replays the stored outcome; divergent reuse
conflicts. A new attempt after a terminal customer cancellation receives a new intent identity and
cannot reuse an old checkout session.

Payment intent, provider payment, commercial satisfaction and relationship activation are separate
state families. Implementations must preserve this progression without a mutable generic `paid` flag:

```text
CHECKOUT_INTENT_CREATED
  -> PROVIDER_CONFIGURATION_PENDING
  -> RAZORPAY_SESSION_CREATED -> CUSTOMER_ACTION_PENDING
       -> PROVIDER_PROCESSING -> CAPTURED | FAILED | EXPIRED | OUTCOME_UNRESOLVED
       -> CUSTOMER_CANCELLED | EXPIRED
  -> ZERO_PRICE_SATISFIED
  -> COMMERCIAL_CONFLICT

CAPTURED | ZERO_PRICE_SATISFIED
  -> ACTIVATION_PENDING -> ACTIVATED | ACTIVATION_UNRESOLVED
```

`CAPTURED` is WBE's signature-verified and reconciled provider-payment fact.
`ZERO_PRICE_SATISFIED` is WBE's promotion-derived no-payment fact. Neither is a BP relationship state,
and neither alone means the relationship is active. An ordinary Checkout dismissal is a browser
observation until the server reconciles provider state; only an explicit customer cancellation of the
bound intent may become `CUSTOMER_CANCELLED`.

Webhook ingestion uses the raw request bytes, accepted Razorpay signature algorithm/secret, provider
event identity and an append-only receipt/inbox. Identical signed replay returns the stored outcome;
same event identity with different bytes is a security conflict. Events may arrive late or out of
order, so WBE reconciles the exact provider order/payment/subscription before advancing state. A
browser callback is an untrusted prompt to query status and never supplies payment truth.

`PROVIDER_CONFIGURATION_PENDING`, `COMMERCIAL_CONFLICT`, `FAILED`, `CUSTOMER_CANCELLED` and `EXPIRED`
intents cannot later be revived into a hosted session. After readiness or commercial facts change,
the customer receives a fresh quote and creates a new intent; prior lineage remains available for
reconciliation and audit without authorizing another order.

### 7.5 Demo 100-Percent Discount And Gateway-Like Experience

The Demo discount is an environment-bound promotion owned by WBE and orchestrated through BP. It is
not a public coupon secret, a payment webhook imitation or a browser-provided bypass flag.

Required semantics:

1. The Founder-approved promotion record names environment `DEMO`, eligible offering/professional,
   discount `100%`, currency, validity interval, use limits, actor/tenant eligibility, stackability,
   authority reference and audit reason.
2. BP requests a commercial quote/order from WBE using server-derived environment, relationship,
   accepted contract and offering. The browser may submit a customer-entered promotional code only
   where a separate public promotion policy allows it; it never submits a bypass authority.
3. WBE validates eligibility and returns an itemized zero-payable commercial outcome retaining list
   price, discount, tax treatment, wallet seed/allowance, renewal price/consequence and promotion ID.
4. The Customer Portal renders the same WAOOAW checkout shell, itemization, `Razorpay is used when an
  amount is due` disclosure and standard method-family gallery used before a paid launch. Credit
  card, debit card, UPI, netbanking and wallet choices are visible as familiar payment options but
  clearly marked `Not required - 100% Demo discount applied`; they are non-editable and collect no
  credentials.
5. A zero-payable order does not launch Razorpay Checkout, create a Razorpay order, request a payment
  method or fabricate a `payment.captured` event. WBE records a distinct `ZERO_PRICE_SATISFIED`
  activation-eligible outcome with idempotency and evidence.
6. BP presents the discount and future renewal consequence before explicit customer confirmation.
   Zero price is not zero consent and does not accept the Employment Contract.
7. Activation consumes the same canonical activation tuple and exactly-once orchestration used for a
   paid capture. It creates one relationship activation, one subscription/wallet outcome and no
   charge.
8. Expired, ineligible, reused, changed or unavailable promotion truth fails closed without falling
   through to a surprise charge or silently changing contract terms.
9. Demo/UAT-only policy cannot be replayed in Production. Environment identity is trusted platform
   context and included in evidence, not caller input.

The fully discounted experience must not mislead the customer into believing a bank, card network,
UPI app, wallet or Razorpay processed money. The completion receipt states `Amount paid: INR 0`,
`100% Demo discount`, `No payment method charged` and the separately governed renewal consequence.

### 7.6 Trial Conversion

Trial conversion preserves relationship and instance identity but creates a new explicit live
authority/commercial snapshot. Trial allowance, synthetic data, demonstration approvals and
non-consequential permissions cannot authorize live provider access or external action. Conversion
does not repeat already confirmed non-stale context, but every live-only scope, credential, budget and
consequence requires its own current confirmation.

### 7.7 Payment Availability And Operability

Razorpay readiness affects only new positive-payable checkout and payment reconciliation. It must not
disable login, Marketplace browsing, existing relationship reads, evidence access, customer data
export, already-authorized non-payment work, pause or Emergency Stop. An unresolved renewal may apply
only the accepted renewal-failure policy; a generic gateway outage cannot silently suspend an agent.

The existing WBE and razorpay-mcp boundaries expose internal health/readiness sufficient to
distinguish configuration missing, authentication rejected, provider unavailable, webhook unhealthy,
reconciliation backlog and healthy operation without exposing credentials or merchant internals.
Operational telemetry includes bounded counts and latency for checkout-intent creation, provider
launch, callback observation, verified webhook, reconciliation, capture, failure, expiry,
zero-price satisfaction and activation handoff. Alerts use pseudonymous correlation and exclude
amounts, customer content and payment credentials unless an approved financial operations surface
requires separately protected detail.

Before account details arrive, deterministic Razorpay emulator/contract fixtures exercise hosted
session creation, method eligibility, callback, signed webhook, duplicate/out-of-order delivery,
timeout, cancellation and reconciliation. Emulator evidence is never labelled provider acceptance.
After details arrive, an authorized test-mode ceremony proves account ownership, expected merchant
display, order/subscription mapping, each enabled method family, callback/webhook receipt, signature,
reconciliation and secret rotation. Live mode remains a separate Founder-authorized gate.

## 8. Personalization, Configuration And Induction

### 8.1 Onboard

Onboard remains completable in approximately two minutes and may change only admitted presentation
preferences: customer-approved display name, visual/chat presentation, locale, accessibility and
timestamp visibility. It cannot alter prompts, model tier, professional persona obligations, Skill
behavior, authority, evidence, pricing, lifecycle, image or professional version.

### 8.2 Induct

Induct is an agent-led, consultative conversation using the admitted professional's induction schema.
It must demonstrate relevant professional understanding, ask only unresolved material questions,
adapt vocabulary to the customer's domain, and show a concise running understanding for correction.

Every context item is classified as:

| Classification | Meaning | Operational use |
|---|---|---|
| `OBSERVED` | Obtained from a named permitted source | May inform a proposal; cannot become customer-confirmed fact by itself. |
| `CUSTOMER_STATED` | Supplied by an authorized participant | Retains speaker/time/source; material use may still require confirmation. |
| `INFERRED` | Derived by the professional with disclosed basis/confidence | Cannot authorize action or silently become confirmed. |
| `MISSING` | Required meaning is not available | Blocks only dependent stages/work and prompts the minimum necessary question. |
| `CONFIRMED` | Explicitly accepted by the authorized customer against an exact proposal revision | May enter the current context snapshot subject to freshness and purpose. |
| `DISPUTED` | Accuracy or interpretation is challenged | Dependent use is blocked until corrected/resolved. |
| `SUPERSEDED` | Replaced by a newer confirmed revision | Historical only; cannot support new work. |

The universal induction command family must support: get current induction, propose context revision,
confirm exact revision, correct exact item/revision, resume conversation and obtain completion/blocker
state. Confirmation requires expected revision and idempotency; divergent replay conflicts. Raw
customer payload remains erasable. Confirmation/correction evidence stores minimum non-reconstructive
proof and append-only lineage.

### 8.3 DMA Release 1 Induction

DMA Skill 0 supplies the domain extension for Release 1. The minimum confirmed context includes:

- business identity, service domain, locality/city and customer-domain vocabulary;
- target audience and excluded audiences;

## 9. Skills, Goals And Operational Eligibility

### 9.1 Skill Binding

The customer selects only Skills declared by the exact ACTIVE admission. Each `SkillInstance` binds
one relationship/instance to an admitted Skill/version, applicability decision, configuration
revision, goal revisions, schedule/review policy, authority state and lifecycle status. A disabled or
inapplicable Skill remains visible with a truthful reason; it is not deleted or silently substituted.

For DMA Release 1, the operational proof remains exactly:

1. Skill 0 - customer profiling and confirmed context;
2. Skill 1 - cited market/digital-presence research; and
3. Skill 2 - proposed strategy requiring customer approval.

No later DMA Skill, provider credential, publication, campaign execution or ad spend is included.

### 9.2 Goal Contract

Every operational Skill requires at least one current goal unless its admitted definition contains an
explicit approved operational-purpose exemption. A goal revision contains:

- goal and Skill identity/version;
- customer-language outcome statement;
- baseline and source, including `BASELINE_NEEDED` where truthful;
- measure definition/version, target or decision threshold and unit;
- evidence sources, attribution basis/limits and uncertainty;
- review cadence and observation window, distinct from execution schedule;
- Decision Space, approvals, budget ceiling and stop conditions;
- customer verifier, verification time/evidence and effective interval; and
- predecessor/successor lineage and materiality result.

The professional may propose. BP validates schema and ownership. The customer verifies. CE validates
governed boundaries/evidence. No model, adapter or browser can verify a goal on the customer's behalf.

### 9.3 Operations Eligibility

BP computes eligibility from current authoritative inputs. At minimum, Operations remains locked if:

- relationship mode/state is ineligible, stopped, suspended, expired or unresolved;
- the professional/admission/artifact/contract stack is incompatible or unavailable;
- required induction context is missing, stale, disputed or unconfirmed;
- a selected Skill is inactive, incompatible or lacks required configuration;
- a required goal is absent, unverified, stale or needs renewed verification;
- Decision Space, budget, commercial readiness, assurance or approval is insufficient;
- a material amendment or open reassessment affects the requested work; or
- CE, PR, WBE or another required owner cannot establish its fail-safe precondition.

Eligibility names every blocking reason and the next authorized action. It never exposes private
topology and never treats unavailable as failed, zero or complete.

## 10. Operations, Performance Monitoring And Improvement

### 10.1 Universal Review Window

Every active Skill has a versioned review policy. Thirty days is the default review cadence only
where the admitted Skill does not require another cadence; it is never a universal execution
schedule. Event-triggered review occurs on material goal/context/authority changes, repeated failure,
quality degradation, customer dispute, Stop, budget exhaustion, provider loss or incompatible
contract/version change.

## 14. Security And Privacy Contract

1. Tenant comes only from the validated server session; relationship/role/authority is resolved on
   every read and command. IDs, cursors and links grant no access.
2. BP authorizes before existence or detailed failure disclosure. Cross-tenant and cross-relationship
   denial is privacy-indistinguishable.
3. Consequential commands bind actor, tenant, relationship, instance, purpose, role, assurance,
   expected revisions, typed acknowledgement where required and idempotency intent.
4. Contract acceptance, paid/zero-price activation, scope expansion, provider credential connection,
   authority changes and approved migrations use the existing fresh-assurance floors.
5. Browser responses are `no-store`; service workers and durable browser stores contain no protected
   lifecycle, context, goal, payment, evidence, assurance or mandate data.
6. Internal calls use mutually authenticated workload identity plus least-privilege delegated purpose.
   Customer bearer tokens and network location are insufficient service authorization.
7. Razorpay card, debit-card, bank, UPI PIN, OTP and wallet credentials remain wholly provider-hosted.
  WAOOAW applies a restrictive script/content-security policy, validates allowed checkout origins,
  protects callback state and verifies webhook signatures over raw bytes before reconciliation.
8. Razorpay merchant key secret and webhook secret remain in the approved secret store with separate
  environment bindings, least-privilege access, rotation evidence and redacted diagnostics. The
  public checkout key is never treated as proof of readiness or authority.
9. Provider credentials never enter induction context, portal payloads, agent images, prompts, logs or
   constitutional evidence. Only opaque readiness/reference meaning crosses the owner boundary.
10. Observability excludes customer content, secrets, raw identifiers and payment credentials. It uses
   approved pseudonymous correlation and bounded reason codes.
11. Emergency Stop bypasses ordinary queues, rate limits, payment, step-up and dependency availability;
   resume requires fresh explicit relationship authority.
12. Prompt injection, malicious documents and customer instructions cannot override the inherited
    Constitution, DCM, admitted Skill schema, Decision Space, tool allowlist or data-purpose boundary.
