# Work Contract 066 — Customer And Employed-Agent Oversight

**Program:** WC-064 — Founder Commercial Governance Program Design
**Iteration:** 2
**Status:** PLANNED OUTCOME CANDIDATE — DETAILED GROOMING AND IMPLEMENTATION UNAUTHORIZED
**Depends on:** WC-065 delivered evidence and independently reviewed learning

## Outcome

Give the Founder an evidence-backed review of whether an active employment relationship is
delivering a responsible combination of customer outcomes, resource sufficiency, selected skills,
provider health, customer participation, agent quality, and economics.

## Objective And Success Measures

Deliver one evidence-backed employment review that separates observed outcome, resource
sufficiency, customer participation, professional quality, provider behavior, constitutional
interruption, and economics. Success requires the Founder to see what is known, what is inferred,
what remains unresolved, and which owner may act next without presenting correlation as causation.

The review must produce `HEALTHY`, `WATCH`, `CORRECTION_PROPOSED`, `ESCALATE`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
with a review window, supporting evidence, customer impact, and no direct mutation of employment,
agent, provider, or billing truth.

## Candidate Capability Boundary

- review by customer, employment relationship, offering, plan, skill, goal, and resource envelope;
- distinguish insufficient advertising/resources, unrealistic goals, wrong skill or plan,
  provider variance, failed generations, customer non-participation, agent-quality drift,
  constitutional interruption, and incomplete evidence;
- compare expected and provisional actual economics while preserving WBE truth;
- propose goal, skill, plan, provider, or resource-envelope correction;
- trigger governed agent-learning proposals without modifying agent lifecycle state; and
- keep customer disclosures, confidence, and unresolved causation visible.

## Excluded

Settled reconciliation operations, direct agent retraining, portfolio-wide optimization, support
case management, and outcome guarantees unsupported by cohort evidence.

## Grooming Rule

WC-066 remains at outcome-and-boundary depth until WC-065 supplies real decision and exception
evidence. Its detailed design must reuse WC-064 identities and semantics rather than reinterpret
offering, goal, cost, margin, policy, or evidence concepts.

## First Grooming Trigger And Evidence Sample

Detailed grooming begins only when WC-065 has supplied independently reviewed samples of:

- allowed, calculated-risk, revised, escalated, and blocked offerability decisions;
- version-pinned expected resource and economic envelopes used by active relationships;
- stale/unavailable input and policy-expiry behavior; and
- customer-facing disclosures and evidence references carried into employment.

The Product Owner must select a representative sample containing successful, underperforming,
resource-constrained, customer-non-participation, provider-variance, and insufficient-evidence
cases. Synthetic examples may test contracts but may not be represented as customer proof.

## Decisions To Close During First Grooming

1. Define review cadence, cohort and relationship selection, minimum evidence, and honest
    insufficient-evidence behavior.
2. Assign outcome, participation, quality, provider, resource, constitutional, and economic
    signals to authoritative owners with freshness and confidence semantics.
3. Define diagnostic hypotheses and evidence standards without allowing unsupported causation or
    automated punitive customer profiling.
4. Define which corrections are informational proposals, which require customer choice, which
    enter agent-lifecycle governance, and which require Founder escalation.
5. Specify customer notice and review rights when a goal, plan, skill, provider, or resource
    correction would change the employment bargain.
6. Define how WC-066 learning evidence feeds WC-067 exceptions and WC-068 portfolio review.

## Grooming Deliverables

- Product review questions, cadence, acceptance matrix, and customer-impact language.
- Business definition of healthy employment value and correction alternatives.
- Enterprise and Solution contracts for relationship selection, evidence assembly, diagnosis,
  proposal routing, owner unavailability, and review history.
- Data lineage and retention contract for observations, hypotheses, confidence, decisions,
  proposals, customer responses, and evidence references.
- Security/Constitutional controls for tenant isolation, minimisation, non-exploitation,
  customer rights, agent-lifecycle separation, and evidence-before-success.
- Migration/no-migration decision and independently reviewed implementation traceability matrix.

## Required Acceptance Scenarios

| Scenario | Required result |
|---|---|
| Outcome and resources on plan | `HEALTHY` with supporting evidence and next review date |
| Resource insufficiency | Correction proposal identifies the envelope gap without blaming agent or customer |
| Customer non-participation | Evidence and confidence remain visible; no punitive inference or hidden downgrade |
| Provider or generation variance | Owner-specific proposal or WC-067 escalation; no direct provider mutation |
| Quality concern | Governed learning/lifecycle proposal; Founder View cannot rewrite the agent |
| Conflicting or incomplete evidence | `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or `WATCH`; no fabricated diagnosis |
| Customer-impacting correction | Notice, choice, and prospective effect are explicit |
| Cross-tenant, stale authority, or evidence failure | Deny/fail closed with no data leakage or success assertion |
