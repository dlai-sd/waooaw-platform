## Constitutional Discovery That Triggered This Amendment

During the onboarding of the RepoNav agent (AVD-001-RepoNav), the Business Architect encountered a constitutional gap:

Article IX of the Constitution defines rights for "Customers" — but assumes the customer is a natural person (an individual who hires a digital professional). RepoNav's customers are engineering organizations — legal entities that hire digital professionals on behalf of their teams.

The following rights in Article IX were found to be undefined for organizational customers:
- *"The right to override any digital professional action at any time without justification"* — which individual within the organization holds this right?
- *"The right to audit their Customer Evidence Ledger completely"* — who in the organization may audit? All employees? Only designated administrators?
- *"The right to terminate any digital professional engagement immediately"* — who in the organization may terminate?
- *"The right to appeal any Permission Engine decision"* — who is the appellant?

Without this amendment, every B2B agent onboarding will encounter the same undefined territory. RepoNav is the first B2B agent; it will not be the last.

## Amendment Text

### A001.1 — Definition of Organizational Customer

An **Organizational Customer** is a legal entity (company, institution, government body, or other incorporated organization) that enters into a WAOOAW Employment Contract on behalf of its human members.

An Organizational Customer holds all the same rights as an individual Customer under Article IX, exercised through an **Authorized Representative**.

### A001.2 — Authorized Representative

An Authorized Representative is a natural person designated by the Organizational Customer in the WAOOAW Employment Contract who holds the Customer rights on the organization's behalf.

**Designation requirements:**
- At least one Authorized Representative must be named at contract inception
- The Organizational Customer may designate up to five Authorized Representatives
- Any Authorized Representative may exercise any Customer right independently — there is no joint-authorization requirement for routine Customer rights
- Change of Authorized Representative requires a written contract amendment — it is not a platform configuration change

**If no Authorized Representative is designated:** The contract's signatory is deemed the Authorized Representative until explicitly changed.

### A001.3 — Employee Rights Within Organizational Customers

Employees of an Organizational Customer are not themselves "Customers" under the WAOOAW Constitution. However, they hold the following rights through their employer's contract:
- The right to use the digital professional within the scope defined by the Organizational Customer's Employment Contract
- The right to invoke Emergency Stop — any employee who has platform access may trigger Emergency Stop without requiring Authorized Representative approval
- The right to receive honest limitation disclosures (C-049) — the digital professional's obligation to disclose limitations applies to all users, not only Authorized Representatives

**Emergency Stop exception:** Emergency Stop is constitutional and cannot be restricted by the Organizational Customer. Any employee with platform access may stop the digital professional at any time. This right may not be removed from the Employment Contract.

### A001.4 — Customer Evidence Ledger for Organizational Customers

The Customer Evidence Ledger for an Organizational Customer is owned by the Organizational Customer as a legal entity — not by any individual employee or Authorized Representative.

**Audit rights:** Any Authorized Representative may audit the Customer Evidence Ledger in full.
**Portability:** The Organizational Customer may request a portable export of their Customer Evidence Ledger. The export is delivered to an Authorized Representative.
**On contract termination:** The Customer Evidence Ledger is transferred to the Organizational Customer per the portability provision. Retention by WAOOAW beyond the contractual retention period is prohibited.

### A001.5 — Organizational Customer Grievance

When an Organizational Customer files a grievance or appeal:
- The grievance is filed by an Authorized Representative
- The investigation and resolution are communicated to the Authorized Representative
- Findings from the grievance are communicated to all Authorized Representatives

### A001.6 — No Reduction of Individual Rights

This amendment does not reduce any right held by the individual Authorized Representatives. It extends the Customer rights framework to organizational entities. Where ambiguity exists, the interpretation that better protects the individual shall be preferred.

## What This Amendment Enables

With this amendment ratified:
1. RepoNav's AVD-001 §11 (Institution Charter Parameters) can specify how the right to override works in organizational context
2. Future B2B agents can be onboarded without encountering this constitutional gap
3. The Employment Contract template for organizational customers can be defined

## What This Amendment Does NOT Change

- Individual Customer rights under Article IX are unchanged
- The three-ledger model (Article VI) is unchanged by this amendment
- The Emergency Stop constitutional guarantee is unchanged
