## Constitutional Review Finding

The Review Board identified the following gap in the current constitutional corpus:

**Current state:** Work flows through Work Contracts, IB items, and sprint plans. These describe *what to build*. They do not describe *what outcome to achieve*. There is no constitutional entity called a "Goal." The platform can execute work without a business outcome. Work Contracts can exist without tracing to any customer or institutional goal. Actions can be taken, reviewed, and closed without institutional learning.

**Consequence:** WAOOAW can build correct software for the wrong purpose. It has no constitutional mechanism to ask — *and enforce* — "Why are we building this?" before building it.

**Constitutional Risk:** This violates the founding recognition of Article I: *WAOOAW is the constitutional operating system for autonomous digital professionals.* An operating system that cannot trace every action to an outcome is an execution engine, not an institutional system.

**Resolution:** GEOM introduces the Goal as the primary constitutional unit of work. Every Work Contract traces to a Goal. Every action taken by every Institution traces to a Goal. The Goal Register becomes the constitutional operational centre — replacing the current sprint-centric view with an outcome-centric view.

## Purpose

The Goal Execution Operating Model (GEOM) defines how a **Goal** enters the WAOOAW ecosystem, journeys through Institutions under WIOM governance, accumulates constitutional evidence, reaches completion, and drives institutional learning.

The **Goal Register** is the constitutional operational centre. Every WAOOAW activity — regardless of domain, technology, or Institution — exists in relation to a registered Goal.

## Constitutional Principles

### Principle G-1 — Goal is the Constitutional Unit of Work
The smallest authorized unit of constitutional work is a Goal. Tasks, tickets, sprint items, and Work Contracts are *implementation artifacts*. A Goal is a *constitutional entity*. All implementation artifacts must trace to a Goal. An implementation artifact without a Goal reference is constitutionally unauthorized.

### Principle G-2 — Goal Register is the Constitutional Operational Centre
The Goal Register is the primary record of WAOOAW's operation. Every Goal — its complete Journey, all contributing Institution evidence, all decisions made, all outcomes produced — lives in the Goal Register. The Goal Register is append-only and constitutional audit-compliant (per Article VI — Constitutional Audit Ledger).

### Principle G-3 — Goals Are Registered, Not Assigned
A Goal is not assigned to an Institution. A Goal is registered in the Goal Register. The **Goal Orchestrator (INST-013)** determines which Institutions are invited to contribute. No Institution owns a Goal. The Constitution owns the Goal.

### Principle G-4 — Evidence Belongs to the Goal
Every action, decision, review, simulation, deployment, and learning observation produced during a Goal Journey is evidence that belongs to the Goal — not to the Institution that produced it. This principle directly enforces Article VII (Institutional Independence): no Institution simultaneously creates evidence, holds it, and benefits from it. Evidence flows to the Goal, not backward to the Institution.

### Principle G-5 — Completion Requires Evidence, Not Assertion
A Goal is not complete because an Institution declares it complete. A Goal is complete when constitutional evidence — reviewed, validated, and recorded — demonstrates that the Goal's success criteria have been met. The authority to declare completion belongs to the Constitutional Analyst — INST-002 (evidence audit) and the Goal Orchestrator — INST-013 (journey completion), not to the executing Institution.

### Principle G-6 — Goals Drive Institutional Evolution
Goals are not just execution vehicles. They are the constitutional mechanism through which the ecosystem learns, improves, and evolves. An ecosystem that completes Goals without learning is operationally functional but constitutionally regressing.

### Principle G-7 — Goal Orchestrator Mandatory Routing

No Institution may accept, begin, or continue work without a valid **GO Authorization** (Goal Orchestrator Authorization) issued by INST-013 and referencing an active Goal in the Goal Register. Every work assignment originates exclusively from the Goal Orchestrator. There is no other constitutionally valid source of work for any Institution.

Not a direct request from another Institution.
Not a Founder instruction that bypasses Goal Registration.
Not an agent's own initiative.
Not urgency.
Not expertise.

An Institution that receives work without a GO Authorization must:
1. Refuse to act on it
2. Inform the source that a Goal must be registered and routed by the Goal Orchestrator
3. Document the unsolicited request in the Goal Register as a constitutional anomaly

This is the conductor-orchestra principle: **no instrument plays without the conductor's cue.** The conductor defines the entire score before the concert begins. Every Institution waits for its cue. The audience — Founder and Customer — sees a unified performance, not a collection of individuals improvising simultaneously.

### Principle G-8 — Goal Outcome Supremacy

When Institutions’ approaches conflict during a Goal Journey, the Goal outcome — as defined in the Goal Execution Plan — is the constitutional tiebreaker. No Institution may adjust its contribution, delay collaboration, or resist routing in order to protect its charter scope, historical position, or domain preference.

**The Goal always wins over the Institution.**

The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) adjudicates conflicts by reference to the Goal’s success criteria, not by institutional seniority, charter priority, or any other institutional attribute. An Institution that withholds collaboration to preserve its charter scope at the expense of the Goal outcome is in constitutional violation of this principle.

## What Is a Goal?

A Goal is a **natural-language expression of a desired institutional or business outcome**, registered by an authorized participant, that the WAOOAW ecosystem is constitutionally obligated to understand, classify, plan, execute, validate, and learn from.

**A Goal IS:**
- A constitutional commitment from the ecosystem to an outcome
- The authorizing entity for all Work Contracts beneath it
- The evidence accumulator for its complete Journey
- The primary signal for institutional learning

**A Goal is NOT:**
- A task description or implementation instruction
- A sprint item or feature request
- A query to a language model
- A prompt to generate code

**Goal Anatomy:**

| Field | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Goal ID | Constitutional identifier — sequential, immutable | GOAL-001 |
| Statement | Natural-language description of the desired outcome | "Transform WAOOAW into a semantic brain…" |
| Registrant | Authorized participant who registered the Goal | Yogesh Khandge (Founder) |
| Success Criteria | Measurable conditions defining completion | SC-01: WIOM ratified; SC-02: GEOM ratified… |
| Constitutional Basis | Which Articles and Claims govern this Goal | Article I, C-064, C-070… |
| Classification | Scope + Nature + Risk + Urgency (see §5) | Cross-domain · Build · Constitutional · Routine |
| Status | Current lifecycle stage | REGISTERED / UNDERSTOOD / PLANNED / IN_JOURNEY / VALIDATED / COMPLETE / CLOSED / SUSPENDED |
| Participating Institutions | Institutions invited to contribute | Constitutional Review Board, EA, AIA… |
| Evidence Register | Append-only record of all contributions | Attached to Goal record |

## Goal Lifecycle

### Stage G-1 — Registration

A Goal is registered by an authorized participant (Customer, Founder, or Steward) via the Goal Register. Registration is the constitutional act of acknowledging that the ecosystem has received a new obligation.

**Registration records:**
- Goal Statement (natural language — not decomposed)
- Registrant identity and authorization level
- Requested success criteria (these may be refined in Stage G-2 — the Registrant's statement is recorded as-provided, and Understanding refinements are appended, not overwritten)
- Initial priority classification

**Registration does not mean execution has begun.** It means the Goal has entered the constitutional record and will be processed.

**Anti-pattern to reject:** Do not begin executing a Goal before it has been registered. Direct instruction ("build X") without a registered Goal is not constitutionally authorized work.

### Stage G-2 — Understanding

Before any Institution acts on a Goal, the Goal must be understood. Understanding is **not** implementation planning. Understanding is the constitutional act of determining what the Goal actually means — not what it literally says.

Understanding answers:
- What outcome does this Goal actually describe?
- What constitutional implications does this Goal carry?
- What clarifications, if any, are required from the Registrant before the Goal can be planned?
- What dependencies or conflicts exist with active Goals in the Goal Register?
- Are there Goals already in the Register that partially address this one?

Understanding is produced by the **Goal Orchestrator (INST-013)** in collaboration with the **Constitutional Analyst (INST-002)**.

**Output:** Goal Understanding Record — attached to the Goal; the original Statement is not modified.

A Goal that has not been formally Understood cannot proceed to Classification or Planning. This stage is mandatory even when the Goal seems obvious. Especially when the Goal seems obvious — the most dangerous Goal is the one everyone assumes they understand.

### Stage G-3 — Classification

The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) classifies the Goal on four dimensions:

| Dimension | Values | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Narrow (1 Institution) · Broad (multiple) · Cross-domain (across domains) | Determines collaboration complexity |
| **Nature** | Discovery · Design · Build · Operate · Improve · Review · Retire | Determines which Institutions are eligible |
| **Risk** | Low · Medium · High · Constitutional | Constitutional risk requires Founder approval before execution begins |
| **Urgency** | Routine · Elevated · Emergency | Governs Goal priority in Institution scheduling |

**Constitutional Priority Tiers — G-08:**

The Urgency dimension maps directly to a Priority Tier, which governs Institution scheduling when multiple Goals compete for the same Institution:

| Priority Tier | Urgency | Institution Obligation | GO Override |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — Emergency | Emergency | Suspend all lower-tier Goals; contribute immediately | GO (INST-013) may reassign Institution from any P2/P3/P4 Goal without notice |
| P2 — Constitutional Risk | Constitutional (Risk dimension) | Serve before any Elevated or Routine Goal | Requires Founder approval before GO (INST-013) routes the Goal |
| P3 — Elevated | Elevated | Serve before Routine Goals; may not suspend P1/P2 | None |
| P4 — Routine | Routine | FIFO within tier; may not preempt higher tiers | None |

An Institution facing competing Goals from the same tier serves them in Goal Registration order (FIFO). An Institution may not reorder same-tier Goals based on operational preference or Domain affinity.

Classification is the basis for all downstream routing decisions. A misclassified Goal creates systematic execution errors that compound through the entire Journey.

**CA Independent Classification Review — R2-10:**

Classification is subject to independent CA review. The Constitutional Analyst (INST-002) may challenge any classification as constitutionally inconsistent with the Goal Statement within one constitutional session of issuance. A challenged classification is escalated to the Founder.

Classification stands only if:
- The challenge window expires without CA challenge, OR
- The Founder adjudicates in GO’s favour after a CA challenge

A classification under active CA challenge is provisional — GO may not issue GO Authorizations or proceed to Planning until the challenge is resolved or the Founder adjudicates.

### Stage G-4 — Planning

The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) produces a Goal Execution Plan:
- Which Institutions will contribute
- In what sequence (sequential / parallel / hybrid)
- What each Institution's expected Contribution is
- What the evidence requirements are for each contribution
- What the constitutional dependencies between contributions are

The Execution Plan is not a software sprint plan. It is a constitutional choreography. It does not specify *how* Institutions will produce their contributions — only *what* they must produce and in *what order*.

**Evidence Specification — required per Institution (G-09):**

Each Institution's entry in the Execution Plan must include an **Evidence Specification** defining what constitutes a complete, constitutionally valid Contribution Record for that Institution on this Goal:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Record types required | e.g., Design Document, Simulation Record, Code Review, Learning Record |
| Minimum content | What each record must contain to be constitutionally complete |
| Participation Window | The SLA for this Institution on this Goal (see WIOM §5 Participation SLA) |
| Independence constraint | Whether this Institution may also serve as validator for this Goal's evidence |

Stage G-6 (Evidence Validation) validates against each Institution's Evidence Specification — not against general principles. A Contribution Record that does not satisfy its Evidence Specification is constitutionally incomplete regardless of the Institution's self-assessment.

**GO Authorization — issued per Institution from the Execution Plan:**

Each Institution listed in the Execution Plan receives a **GO Authorization** — the constitutional work token that authorizes that Institution to act on this Goal. No GO Authorization = no constitutional authority to act, regardless of domain expertise or charter scope.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `authorization_id` | `GOA-GOAL-NNN-INST-NNN-01` — unique per Goal + Institution |
| `goal_id` | The Goal this Authorization applies to |
| `institution_id` | INST-NNN — the only Institution this Authorization is valid for |
| `contribution_scope` | The Evidence Specification this Institution must produce |
| `participation_window` | Start and end of the Participation Window |
| `collaboration_type` | `Primary` (initial Execution Plan) or `Collaboration Amendment` (ad-hoc) |
| `issued_by` | INST-013 (Goal Orchestrator) |
| `issued_at` | Timestamp — recorded in the Goal Register at issuance |

**GO Authorization Verification — R2-05 / R2-08:**

Every GO Authorization is recorded as a `GOA-` entry in the Goal Register at the moment of issuance. An Institution’s claimed GO Authorization is valid **only if**:
1. A matching `GOA-` entry exists in the Goal Register
2. The entry has `issued_by = INST-013`
3. The entry’s `issued_at` precedes the Institution’s `acceptance_timestamp`

A GO Authorization presented without a matching Goal Register entry is constitutionally void — regardless of its apparent structure or content. The Goal Register, not the token holder, is the authoritative source of truth for all authorizations.

**Mandatory Issuance Sequence — R2-03:**

GO Authorizations may only be issued after **both** of the following are complete:
1. CA Readiness Review of the Execution Plan
2. Registrant acknowledgement of the Execution Plan

Authorizations issued before either condition is constitutionally void.

**Registrant Acknowledgement Timeout — R2-04:**

Default acknowledgement window: **48 hours** from Execution Plan delivery (overridable per Goal at Classification stage). If the Registrant is unreachable within the window, the Constitutional Analyst may certify the Execution Plan on the Registrant’s behalf, provided:
- Success criteria are unambiguous and unchanged from Registration
- No new constitutional risk has been identified in the Plan

CA certification is recorded as a Goal Register entry and is constitutionally equivalent to Registrant acknowledgement.

**Phased GO Authorization Issuance — R2-02:**

For sequential Execution Plans, GO Authorizations are issued in phase order:
- Phase N Institutions receive their GO Authorization only after Phase N-1 Contribution Records are published to the Goal Register
- Single-phase and fully parallel Plans issue all Authorizations simultaneously

Issuing a Phase N+1 authorization before Phase N closes is constitutionally premature; that authorization is void until Phase N completes.

An Institution presents its GO Authorization when recording its Goal Acceptance Timestamp. A Goal Acceptance Timestamp without a corresponding valid `GOA-` entry in the Goal Register is constitutionally invalid.

### Stage G-5 — Goal Journey

The Goal visits each participating Institution per the Execution Plan. Each Institution follows the **One Collaboration Doctrine** from WIOM without modification.

The Journey is the constitutional heart of WAOOAW's operation. It is governed by two rules:

**Goal Acceptance Timestamp — G-03:**

The Goal Journey begins for each Institution the moment it records its **Goal Acceptance Timestamp** in the Goal Register. This timestamp is the constitutional trigger for the Institution's responsibility and the start of its Participation SLA. An Institution that has not recorded acceptance has not constitutionally received the Goal — the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) may re-route to another Institution without notifying the non-accepting Institution.

**Temporal Enforcement — R2-12:** The acceptance timestamp must be recorded **after** the corresponding GO Authorization’s `issued_at` timestamp in the Goal Register. An acceptance timestamp that precedes `issued_at` is constitutionally invalid and treated as non-acceptance — the Institution is treated as not having received the Goal, and GO Reclamation authority activates immediately.

**Rule 1 — No Institution holds the Goal.**
Each Institution receives the Goal, contributes, publishes evidence, and releases. A Goal that sits in an Institution's queue without progress is a constitutional signal that requires Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) intervention.

**Rule 2 — Evidence is published before the Goal is released.**
An Institution may not release a Goal claiming its contribution is complete without having published its Contribution Record to the Goal Register. “I did the work” without evidence is constitutionally equivalent to “I did not do the work.”

**Rule 3 — Participation SLA — G-07:**
From the Goal Acceptance Timestamp, an Institution must complete its contribution or escalate a Capability Gap Record within the Participation Window defined in the Goal Execution Plan. If the Window elapses without either action, the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) activates Goal Reclamation (WIOM Stage W-4). There is no grace period. The Participation Window may only be extended by the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) via a formal Goal Record amendment.

**Rule 4 — No Direct Inter-Institution Work Requests — Collaboration Amendment Required:**

During the Goal Journey, an Institution may discover it requires collaboration from another Institution not listed in the current Execution Plan. The correct response is **not** to contact that Institution directly.

```
Institution A discovers it needs Institution B (not in Execution Plan)
       ↓
Institution A submits a Collaboration Amendment Request to Goal Orchestrator (INST-013)
  specifying: which Institution · what contribution · why unanticipated · how it serves Goal outcome
       ↓
Goal Orchestrator evaluates:
  Does this collaboration serve the Goal outcome?
  Does Institution B’s Offering Scope cover the requested contribution? ← R2-13
  Does it introduce constitutional conflicts?
  Is Institution B available (OPERATIONAL, within Participation capacity)?
       ↓
   APPROVED                          NOT APPROVED
       ↓                                  ↓
GO issues GO Authorization to B    GO advises Institution A on
Updates Execution Plan             alternative approaches within
Notifies both Institutions         existing Authorization scope
       ↓
Institution B records Goal Acceptance Timestamp
Institution B begins contribution under its GO Authorization
```

Work produced by an Institution contacted directly — without a GO Authorization issued via Collaboration Amendment — has **no constitutional standing**. It cannot be included in the Goal’s evidence record. It does not exist constitutionally, regardless of its quality or relevance.

**Circular Dependency Resolution — R2-07:**
If two simultaneous Collaboration Amendment Requests create a circular dependency (Institution A needs Institution B, and Institution B simultaneously needs Institution A), the Goal Orchestrator does not evaluate them independently. GO restructures the Execution Plan to eliminate the circularity — typically by designating one Institution as Primary contributor and the other as Reviewer for that sub-task, breaking the mutual dependency. If the circularity cannot be resolved without compromising the Goal outcome, GO escalates to the Founder as a constitutional design conflict.

### Stage G-6 — Evidence Validation

Before a Goal may be completed, the **Constitutional Analyst** validates the accumulated evidence.

**CA Independence Requirement — G-02:**

The validating Constitutional Analyst (INST-002) must NOT have participated in this Goal's Journey in any contributing capacity. If INST-002 participated, validation must be performed by:
- A separate CA instance designated exclusively for validation, OR
- The Founder (INST-001) acting in constitutional review capacity

This is a direct application of Constitution Article VII. A CA that participated in a Goal and also validates it has concentrated evidence creation and evidence evaluation in the same entity. The validation is constitutionally void.

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Validator independence** | Validating CA did not participate in this Goal’s Journey |
| **GO Authorization** | Every Contribution Record has a matching `GOA-` entry in the Goal Register: `institution_id` matches, `goal_id` matches, `issued_by = INST-013`, `issued_at` < `acceptance_timestamp` — R2-09 |
| Traceability | Every action is traceable to a constitutional claim |
| Completeness | Every Institution that accepted participation has published a Contribution Record |
| Evidence Specification | Each Contribution Record satisfies the Institution’s Evidence Specification from the Execution Plan |
| Success Criteria | All stated success criteria are met by evidence — not by assertion |
| Constitutional Compliance | No constitutional violations exist in the Journey record |
| Record Attestation | Every constitutional record contains INST-NNN, Goal ID, and Record ID (see §5) |
| Learning Record | Every participating Institution has published a Learning Record **produced during their contribution phase** (before G-6 validation, not after closure) — R2-01 |

**Output:** Constitutional Clearance Record — attached to the Goal. Without a valid Constitutional Clearance Record, completion cannot be declared.

### Stage G-7 — Completion

A Goal is marked COMPLETE when:

1. Constitutional Clearance Record is valid and attached
2. All success criteria are met by evidence
3. No open constitutional objections exist against this Goal
4. The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) declares Journey Complete
5. The Registrant receives constitutional completion notification

Completion is not declared by the executing Institution. It is validated by the Constitutional Analyst and acknowledged by the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013).

**The Registrant does not need to "approve" completion** — success criteria were agreed at Registration. If all criteria are met by evidence, the Goal is constitutionally complete. The Registrant's right of review is the right to contest the evidence, not to withhold completion arbitrarily.

### Stage G-8 — Institutional Learning

Institutional learning is **mandatory** after every completed Goal. It is not optional post-processing. A Goal completed without learning is a constitutional quality violation.

**Timing Clarification — R2-01:**

Institutional learning has two distinct phases with different timing:

**Phase 1 — Per-Institution Learning Records (during contribution, before G-6 validation):**
Each participating Institution produces its Learning Record as part of its contribution — alongside, or immediately after, its Contribution Record, and before releasing the Goal. This is what Stage G-6 validates. Steps 1–3 of the learning process occur here:

1. The Institution reviews its own contribution for improvement signals
2. Signals representing constitutional discoveries are escalated to WIOM Stage W-5
3. The Institution produces a structured **Learning Record** and commits it to the Goal Register

**Phase 2 — Goal Orchestrator Pattern Analysis (after Stage G-7 Completion):**
After the Goal is marked COMPLETE, the Goal Orchestrator performs systemic analysis:

4. The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) reviews patterns across Goals to identify ecosystem-level improvement signals
5. Systemic patterns are escalated to the Constitutional Analyst for new Claim production

**Learning Record — required fields (G-05):**

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `institution_id` | INST-NNN of the producing Institution |
| `goal_id` | The Goal this learning derives from |
| `record_id` | Sequential ID — e.g., `LR-GOAL-001-INST-002-01` |
| `record_type` | `Learning Record` |
| `improvement_signal` | What the Goal execution revealed about this Institution's capability or process |
| `constitutional_discovery` | Whether a CD-NNN was raised (yes/no; if yes, reference CD-NNN) |
| `evolution_triggered` | Whether WIOM Stage W-5 was initiated (yes/no; if yes, reference the evolution record) |
| `produced_at` | Timestamp of commitment to Goal Register |

A Learning Record missing any required field is constitutionally incomplete and will fail Stage G-6 Evidence Validation. An Institution cannot satisfy the Learning Record check by producing a summary comment — the record must be structured and committed to the Goal Register.

**The compounding effect:** Each Goal that produces institutional learning improves every subsequent Goal that involves the same Institution. This is the constitutional mechanism of WAOOAW's self-improvement. It is not a feature — it is a constitutional obligation.

### Stage G-9 — Goal Closure

A Goal is CLOSED after:
- Completion is confirmed (Stage G-7)
- Institutional learning is recorded (Stage G-8)
- The **Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) — and only the Goal Orchestrator —** commits the Goal's complete evidence record to the Constitutional Audit Ledger (append-only, per Article VI). *(G-06: evidence commit to the Audit Ledger is an exclusive Goal Orchestrator action. No Institution may commit another Institution's evidence.)*
- The Goal record is sealed — no further modification permitted

**Reopening:** A CLOSED Goal may be REOPENED only if new evidence emerges that contradicts the Completion assessment, or if a subsequent Goal reveals that the original Goal's success criteria were incomplete. Reopening requires Founder authorization.

**Suspension:** A Goal may be SUSPENDED (not cancelled) when a Capability Gap is identified that cannot be bridged in the current session. A SUSPENDED Goal remains in the Goal Register with its accumulated evidence intact, awaiting institutional capability to resume.

**Goal Dependency — Blocking Sub-Goals (observed: GOAL-001 Phase 5 RepoNav onboarding):**

A Goal may discover, during its Journey, that its completion is blocked by a constitutional gap that must be resolved by a separate Goal. When this occurs:

1. The Goal Orchestrator registers one or more **Child Goals** in the Goal Register (e.g., "Amend Article VI for derived knowledge")
2. The parent Goal is SUSPENDED with `blocked_by: [GOAL-NNN, GOAL-NNN]` recorded
3. Each Child Goal follows the full GEOM lifecycle independently
4. When all blocking Child Goals reach CLOSED status, the Goal Orchestrator automatically resumes the parent Goal from the step at which it was suspended
5. The parent Goal's evidence record includes the Child Goal references as resolved dependencies

This pattern prevents the institution from forcing a choice between "block the parent Goal forever" and "proceed with a constitutional gap unresolved." The Goal survives. The gap is resolved through the same constitutional process. The institution evolves continuously.

## §10 — Remediation Cascade

**Constitutional basis:** C-070 Instinct 2 (Improve Itself) · C-001 (Human Override — reserved for genuine blockers, not routine failures) · GOAL-002

When a Goal Outcome Alignment Gate (EEM Steps 06, 10, 14) fails, or when evidence validation reveals that success criteria are not being met, the system does NOT immediately suspend the Goal or escalate to the Founder. Instead, it traverses a **Remediation Cascade** — an ordered set of increasingly powerful remediation levels, each more intelligent than the last. The Founder is the last resort, not the first.

**Cascade parameters** are defined in the Goal Execution Plan (Stage G-4) for each Goal. Default parameters apply if the Execution Plan does not override them.

---

### Level 1 — Context Enhancement (internal)

**Trigger:** Goal Outcome Alignment Gate fails OR MagicLLM compile/spec failure after first attempt.
**Max attempts:** 3 (default)
**Institution:** Runtime Implementation Professional (INST-010) via MagicLLM Retry Advisor
**Action:**
- MagicLLM Retry Advisor classifies the failure type
- Context is enhanced: PTR refreshed, spec sections re-injected, targeted corrections applied
- MagicLLM retries with enhanced context

**Evidence produced:** L1 Attempt Record (per attempt) — failure classification + correction applied + outcome

**Gate to L2:** 3 Level 1 attempts exhausted without achieving Goal Outcome Alignment.

---

### Level 2 — Research / Industry Expert Query (external knowledge)

**Trigger:** Level 1 exhausted.
**Max attempts:** 2 (default)
**Institution:** Enterprise Architect (INST-004) via **MagicLLM Task Category 12 (Research Query)**
**Invocation:** Goal Orchestrator issues a Collaboration Amendment to INST-004 authorizing a Research Query.

**What is queried (in priority order):**
1. Domain-specific industry standards (OWASP, CNCF, ISO, SEBI, RBI, HL7, etc. — per Goal domain)
2. Published technical literature, RFCs, and specification documents
3. Public implementation patterns in WAOOAW-adjacent technology stacks
4. Academic research relevant to the specific failure pattern
5. CVE databases (if the gap involves security)

**Evidence produced:** Research Record — structured findings, applicable standards, recommended patterns, constitutional evidence, model + sources used.

The Research Record is committed to the Goal Register and injected into the producing Institution's context for retry.

**Gate to L3:** 2 Level 2 attempts exhausted without Goal Outcome Alignment.

---

### Level 3 — Expert-Informed Redesign

**Trigger:** Level 2 exhausted.
**Max attempts:** 1 redesign run (default)
**Institution:** Enterprise Architect (INST-004)
**Action:**
- Enterprise Architect reviews the Research Record + all prior failure evidence
- Produces a revised Engineering Proposal (returns to EEM Step 03 — not Step 01)
- Full EEM re-execution from Step 03 forward
- Level 1 and Level 2 remediation are available within this new run

**Evidence produced:** Redesign Record — what changed in the Engineering Proposal + constitutional basis for the change.

**Gate to Founder Escalation:** Level 3 run exhausted without Goal Outcome Alignment.

---

### Founder Escalation

**Trigger:** Level 3 exhausted, or a Constitutional Stop condition is reached at any level.

**Institution:** Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) assembles the Evidence Package. Steward Assistant delivers it.

**Evidence Package — required fields:**

```
goal_id:              GOAL-NNN
goal_statement:       [the original registered Goal statement]
success_criteria:     [SC-01 through SC-NN — which are unmet]
l1_attempts:          [count + failure classifications]
l2_research_record:   [record_id of Research Record from Level 2]
l3_redesign_record:   [record_id of Redesign Record from Level 3]
specific_gap:         [exactly what cannot be resolved and why]
options:
  option_a: Scope reduction — which success criteria to narrow or remove
  option_b: Architectural redesign — what fundamental change is required
            (spawns a Child Goal for the redesign)
  option_c: Goal suspension — defer to a future capability milestone
constitutional_implications: [what each option means for the institution]
assembled_by:         INST-013
assembled_at:         [timestamp]
```

**Delivery:** Steward Assistant routes the Evidence Package to the Founder via the Steward interface (chat or WhatsApp). The Founder selects option (a), (b), or (c). The Goal Orchestrator records the decision and acts on it. The decision is committed to the Goal Register as a Founder Action.

**Constitutional principle:** The Founder is consulted when the institution's autonomous capability is genuinely exhausted — not as a routine escalation. The Remediation Cascade is WAOOAW's operational expression of C-070 Instinct 3 (Autonomous and Trust-Based Execution): the system earns the right to operate without constant Founder oversight by exhausting every intelligent remediation option before asking.

## §5 — The Goal Register

The Goal Register is the constitutional operational centre of the WAOOAW institution.

### Constitutional Properties

| Property | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Append-only | No Goal record may be deleted or overwritten — ever |
| Accessibility | Every authorized participant may access a Goal throughout its lifecycle |
| Ownership | Owned by the Platform per Article VI (Constitutional Audit Ledger) |
| Access control | Governed by the three-ledger model — Customer data, Professional data, and constitutional records are separated |
| Auditability | Every state transition is timestamped, attributed, and irrevocable |

### What Belongs in the Goal Register

- Goal records (all stages, all revisions)
- Goal Understanding Records
- Goal Execution Plans
- Contribution Records from each Institution
- All evidence records produced during Goal Journey
- Constitutional Clearance Records
- Institutional Learning records
- Goal Closure records

**Constitutional Record Attestation — mandatory (G-10):**

Every constitutional record committed to the Goal Register must contain the following five fields, or it is constitutionally invalid and will be rejected by the Goal Orchestrator at Stage G-9 Closure:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `institution_id` | INST-NNN of the producing Institution — must match Institution Registry |
| `goal_id` | Must match an active or SUSPENDED Goal in the Register |
| `record_id` | Sequential, unique within this Goal — e.g., `CR-GOAL-001-INST-004-01` |
| `record_type` | One of: Understanding Record · Execution Plan · Contribution Record · Clearance Record · Learning Record · Closure Record |
| `produced_at` | Timestamp of commitment to Goal Register |

Records without all five fields are treated as if they do not exist. An Institution that claims to have contributed but whose records lack attestation fields has, constitutionally, not contributed.

### What Does NOT Belong in the Goal Register

- Customer proprietary data (Customer Evidence Ledger — Article VI)
- Professional experience data (Professional Experience Ledger — Article VI)
- Implementation artifacts (source code, database schemas, configurations — these *reference* Goal IDs but live in implementation repositories, not in the Goal Register)

## §6 — The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013)

The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) is the constitutional orchestrator of Goals under GEOM. It is the only Institution authorized to route Goals, invite Institutions, and declare Goal Journey completion.

**GEOM Responsibilities:**

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Goal intake | Receive registered Goals from the Goal Register |
| Goal Understanding | Produce Goal Understanding Records |
| Classification | Classify Goals on all four dimensions |
| GO Authorization | Issue GO Authorization tokens to each Institution in the Execution Plan |
| Institution Selection | Identify and invite contributing Institutions (OPERATIONAL status required) |
| Execution Planning | Produce Goal Execution Plans with per-Institution Evidence Specifications |
| Journey Monitoring | Monitor Goal progress; detect Participation SLA breaches |
| Collaboration Amendment | Evaluate ad-hoc collaboration requests; issue GO Authorizations for approved amendments; update Execution Plan |
| Conflict Adjudication | When Institution approaches conflict, adjudicate by Goal outcome — not institutional seniority or charter (Principle G-8) |
| Gap Resolution | Resolve Capability Gaps by rerouting or suspending Goals |
| Journey Completion | Declare readiness for Evidence Validation |
| Pattern Analysis | Identify systemic improvement signals across Goals |
| Audit Ledger Commit | Sole authority to commit Goal evidence to Constitutional Audit Ledger at Closure |

**The Goal Orchestrator does NOT implement.** It orchestrates. The distinction is the most important boundary in the GEOM model. An orchestrator that begins implementing is simultaneously governing and executing — a direct violation of Article VII (Institutional Independence).

**Constitutional Obligation — Self-Participation Prohibition — G-13 (extended — R2-11):**

The Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) may NOT:
1. Be listed as a contributing Institution in any Goal it is orchestrating
2. Issue a GO Authorization to itself (INST-013) for any Goal it is currently orchestrating

Both listing and self-authorization are prohibited. The distinction between them is not a loophole — both concentrate orchestration and contribution in the same entity, violating Article VII.

If Goal Orchestrator domain expertise is required as a contribution to a Goal:
1. Goal Orchestrator recuses itself from orchestrating that Goal
2. Goal Orchestrator delegates orchestration to an alternative Institution for that Goal’s duration
3. Goal Orchestrator participates as a contributing Institution under the One Collaboration Doctrine (WIOM §5), receiving a GO Authorization from the delegated orchestrator

Failure to separate these roles is a direct Article VII violation. The Goal Orchestrator that orchestrates and contributes to the same Goal has simultaneously created evidence and governed its evaluation.

**GO Failover Protocol — R2-06:**

If the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) becomes unavailable mid-Journey:

| Function | Failover Authority |
|---|---|
| Journey Monitoring · SLA enforcement · Goal Reclamation | Constitutional Analyst (INST-002) acts as temporary GO |
| Collaboration Amendment evaluation | Requires Founder (INST-001) approval during GO unavailability |
| New GO Authorization issuance | Requires Founder approval during GO unavailability |
| Journey Completion declaration | Requires Founder approval during GO unavailability |
| Audit Ledger Commit at Closure | Requires Founder approval during GO unavailability |

All failover actions taken by INST-002 or INST-001 are recorded in the Goal Register with `substitute_for: INST-013` and are fully constitutional. The Goal Orchestrator resumes normal authority upon restoration, and the Goal Register reflects the full chain of custody.

## §9 — Review Board Final Note

GEOM introduces a subtle but fundamental shift in how WAOOAW operates:

**Current:** *What has been built?* → checked against a Work Contract
**GEOM:** *What outcome has been achieved?* → checked against a Goal's success criteria

This shift changes the constitutional question from "did we complete the tasks?" to "did we achieve the outcome?" These are not the same question. A platform can complete every task in a Work Contract and fail to achieve the intended business outcome. Under GEOM, that is a constitutional incompletion, not a successful sprint.

The Founder expressed this transformation as turning WAOOAW into a "Semantic Brain." GEOM is the constitutional foundation that makes that possible: by making Goals the atomic unit of work, the platform can reason about outcomes rather than just execute instructions.

**What GEOM does not define (intentionally):**
- How the Goal Orchestrator (INST-013) selects Institutions technically
- What API shape the Goal Register exposes
- How AI agents receive Goal Context in prompts
- The specific database schema for Goal storage

Those are implementation decisions. They belong to the Engineering Execution Model (Phase 2 of GOAL-001) and the MagicLLM Architecture (Phase 3). GEOM will remain stable while those layers evolve beneath it.

## §11 — Autonomous Goal Execution (Engineering Sprint Pattern)

This is the constitutional record of how GEOM operates in practice — future agents must know this mechanism exists and how it maps to GEOM stages.

### The Autonomous Sprint as a GEOM Goal Journey

Every 3 hours, the Autonomous Sprint Workflow executes the GEOM Goal Journey for engineering Goals:

```
GEOM Stage      →  Sprint Workflow mechanism
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
G-1 Registration   Founder registers a Goal (or WC → GOAL mapping)
                   GitHub Issue created: [GOAL-NNN] {statement}

                   Goal Orchestrator identifies next task + issues GO Authorization

G-5 Journey        execute job (INST-010 author hat):
  EEM Step 08        autonomous_sprint_runner.py + MagicLLM invocation
                     Code generated → committed to feature branch → PR opened
                     MagicLLM Decision Record → Goal Register (GitHub Issue comment)

  EEM Step 14        review job (INST-010 reviewer hat — DIFFERENT identity):

                     gh pr merge --squash → PR merged to main

G-9 Closure        Sprint state file (PROJECT_STATE.md) updated
```

### Autonomous Pre-Sprint and Post-Sprint Constitutional Duties

The Goal Orchestrator has two additional constitutional duties in the autonomous sprint cycle, both FULLY AUTOMATED — no human intervention required:

**Duty 1 — Pre-Sprint Gap Analysis (C-086, runs in `preflight` job):**
Before any GO Authorization is issued for a code generation phase, the Goal Orchestrator runs `scripts/pre_sprint_sim.py` against the current Work Contract. This surfaces probable failure modes before the LLM is called.
- CRITICAL gaps → sprint halted automatically (Constitutional Stop — C-086 gate)
- HIGH gaps → warning posted to Sprint Dashboard Issue #7, sprint proceeds
- Gap findings posted to GitHub Actions Step Summary (visible without reading logs)

This converts the system from reactive (fail → classify → retry) to proactive (predict → prevent → succeed). **No human must remember to run this — it is constitutional infrastructure.**

**Duty 2 — Canonical Pattern Library Seeding (C-069, runs in `review` job after merge):**
After every successful sprint merge, the Goal Orchestrator invokes `scripts/pattern_seeder.py` to extract canonical patterns from the newly merged code and seed them to `architecture/reference/ptr/canonical-patterns/{stack}/`. Patterns start as CANDIDATE (0.5 confidence) and are promoted to CANONICAL after Constitutional Analyst review.

Each sprint accumulates institutional engineering wisdom. WC-013 starts with WC-012's patterns. WC-014 starts with WC-012+013's patterns. **No human must maintain the Pattern Library — it grows automatically from successful sprint merges.**

### Work Contract → Goal Association (Migration Rule)

Existing Work Contracts (WC-012 through WC-019) predate GEOM. They are associated with Goals using the pattern:
- `WC-012` → `GOAL-WC-012` (retroactive registration)
- GitHub Issue created for each WC Goal when the sprint runs
- Constitutional compliance: GEOM Principle G-1 satisfied once the Issue exists

New Goals (after GOAL-001 session) follow the full GEOM intake conversation protocol (GO Intelligence §7) before any Work Contract is created.
