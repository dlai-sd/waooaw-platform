# AE-01 Business and Professional Boundary Contract

**Producing Institution:** INST-003 — Business Architect
**Authorization:** GOA-GOAL-005-INST-003-02
**Status:** D-06 CONTRIBUTED — implementation-neutral

## Generic Employment Capability

WC-057 through WC-060 implement one generic customer journey for one customer tenant and one professional relationship. Shared capabilities are professional discovery, rights/capability disclosure, interview protocol, progressive context, trial lifecycle, configuration, contract, activation, continuity, evidence, and Stop.

The shared platform does not encode DMA vocabulary, DMA skill IDs, marketing compliance, marketing assets, campaign logic, or marketing recommendations. It invokes a versioned Professional Evaluation Adapter supplied by the selected professional.

## DMA First Proof

The DMA adapter is the first proof fixture and production professional adapter. Its 19 skills, trial plan, Domain Vocabulary Engine, compliance table, and synthetic/free substitutions live in DMA-owned knowledge/configuration and Skill Runtime artifacts. A second professional proves generic extensibility by conforming to the same adapter contract in tests with at least three representative skills; it does not require full second-domain release grooming.

## Wave Boundary

- AE-01 demonstrates proposed behavior and forms one active Employment Relationship.
- AE-02 implements real professional work and provider mutation.
- AE-03 expands ongoing workplace/review/lifecycle behavior.
- AE-04 introduces multiple independently governed professionals and cross-agent handoffs.

Multiple conversations or channels for one relationship are AE-01 continuity, not multi-agent behavior. WC-060 must not aggregate multiple professionals, budgets, authorities, or evidence ledgers.

## Commercial Semantics

`CONVERTED` may exist as a WBE trial billing projection only. It is not an Employment Relationship lifecycle state. Product language is “paid activation,” governed by explicit contract acceptance, activation-eligible payment, and the D-03 tuple.

The successful AE-01 outcome is an informed `HIRE`, `NOT_NOW`, or `EXIT` decision. Conversion rate is a learning metric, never a pressure target or release pass condition.

## Acceptance

Generic code has zero DMA conditionals; DMA first-proof passes all 19 demonstrations; a second adapter conformance fixture passes generic discovery/interview/trial/configuration; no AE-02 action executes; and the customer can decline without losing evidence or approved trial artifacts.
