## 3. Mandatory Anti-Drift Order

By the Founder's explicit instruction, the following conditions are mandatory for every Work
Contract, implementation PR, qualification record, and release claim that states it delivers or
preserves the WAOOAW Customer Portal experience:

1. `CPUI-A01` through `CPUI-A20` are indivisible acceptance obligations. Applicability may be
   narrowed only by an explicit superseding Founder decision that names the affected IDs and reason.
2. A hidden or optional drawer alone cannot satisfy the conversational-workspace obligation.
3. Registration intent must remain distinct from login intent. `REGISTRATION_REQUIRED`, policy
   denial, dependency failure, and expired authentication cannot share misleading customer copy.
4. Avoiding a full-document request does not by itself prove SPA quality. Stable shell identity,
   brand and content geometry, typography, focus, responsive behavior, and bounded layout shift are
   independently required.
5. Unit tests, aggregate suite counts, local fixtures, and isolated screenshots cannot substitute
   for the named end-to-end browser journeys and exact-revision Demo acceptance.
6. Any change to Customer Portal shell, navigation, authentication/registration presentation,
   conversational workspace, or acquisition continuation must identify the affected CPUI IDs in
   its specification traceability and regression evidence.
7. A missing required CPUI result blocks `complete`, `qualified`, `ready`, `accepted`, and equivalent
   claims. Such a claim without matching evidence conflicts with C-002 and C-059 and must be treated
   as a constitutional delivery blocker.

These conditions remain in force for the component until superseded through explicit Founder
authority and the repository's governed specification process. Silence, narrower PR wording,
passing unrelated checks, or a later refactor does not waive them.
