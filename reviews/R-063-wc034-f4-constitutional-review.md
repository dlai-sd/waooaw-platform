## 5. Rights, Authority, And Lifecycle Determination

The package preserves the constitutional independence of the principal customer meanings:

- **Rights** remain visible even when digital exercise is unavailable; Emergency Stop is never hidden by commercial, evidence, degraded-owner, or lifecycle state.
- **Scope** defines included and excluded work and requires a distinct boundary-confirmation event when changed.
- **Authority** identifies who may act, over what scope, for what duration and ceiling, and under which stop conditions. Capability and relationship ownership do not substitute for licensed authority.
- **Lifecycle** uses Evaluation, Active, Suspended, and Terminated with typed work, schedule, evidence, billing, allowance, and re-entry consequences supplied by accountable owners.
- **Approval** grants only the stated next step. It does not silently confirm scope, grant authority, accept a deliverable, terminate a relationship, or prove a result.
- **Boundary confirmation** is a separate C3 command with fresh assurance, typed acknowledgement, current versions, and distinct CE evidence.

These distinctions implement CP-002 and CP-003 and preserve the Bill of Rights, Human Override, and Right of Review. They are constitutionally acceptable provided unresolved owner policies remain blocked as required by CONDITION-2.
