## 1. Scope And Independence

This is the requested lightweight constitutional pass and one bounded write-permission contribution
and repair pass for Solution, Enterprise, Security, Data and Platform Architecture. It does not
claim independent approval: one continuous authoring context synthesized the contributions. It
creates no claim, ADR, implementation authority, deployment authority, PR approval or merge authority.

No unresolved contradiction requiring a Constitutional Blocker was found in the proposed boundary.
The implementation gaps listed in Section 6 remain delivery blockers, not evidence against the model.

## 4. Mandatory Conditions

1. The Agent Authoring Guide Section 9A and Activation Gate Section 17 remain binary requirements.
2. An active Founder-authorized ABP, bundle and Thread mapping is required before offerability.
3. Package entitlement never substitutes for Decision Space, approval or spend authority.
4. Paid provider dispatch cannot occur before reservation or avoid terminal settlement.
5. Stop and mandatory disclosure remain available when allowance or dependencies are exhausted.
6. DMA Release 1 remains limited to Profiling, Research and Strategy for this work component.
7. Customer Portal UI Coherence governs the customer-facing workspace and envelope projection.
8. Canonical PostgreSQL and exact-image evidence are required; handcrafted schemas do not qualify.
9. Final Enterprise acceptance, implementation authorization, deployment and merge remain reserved.

## 5. Verdict

**CONSTITUTIONALLY ALIGNED AS PROPOSED - MANDATORY CONDITIONS APPLY.** The Professional Operating
Envelope correctly places the common institutional organ in WAOOAW while preserving the agent as
the professional decision-maker. Founder acceptance is required before it becomes the reusable
new-agent template; implementation remains separately gated.
