## 1. Founder Direction Incorporated

1. WAOOAW does not select a preferred or ideal customer segment. It offers pre-built professional domains to varied lawful businesses and personalizes each relationship through progressive context.
2. Eligibility is governed by law, regulation, professional rules, platform policy, and constitutional prohibitions. Illegal or exploitative work, including narcotics promotion and child sexual exploitation, is ineligible.
3. DMA's posture is consultative, expert, empathetic, and an advocate for the customer's lawful business outcome.
4. Professional interview questions and trustworthy-answer standards are DMA body-of-knowledge obligations, not questions to return to the Founder or Sujay.
5. The trial demonstrates the full DMA skill set without paid LLM APIs or consequential external action.
6. Minimum initial context is the person's name, location, and nature of business. DMA confirms what it understands, preserves and reuses confirmed context, and progressively asks at most one additional business or personal question per interaction cycle or scheduled review.
7. Each active skill is configured against a customer goal and measure. DMA recommends an evidence-backed review every two months and showcases achievements, misses, learning, and next actions.
8. Trial conversion must earn the customer's decision through demonstrated value while avoiding manipulation and respecting WAOOAW's cost structure.
9. Trial duration is 14 calendar days. DMA plans useful trial activity across the full period; the trial does not terminate after three sessions.
10. DMA owns the professional recommendation for what the customer must learn and decide at AE-01-S01 through S10.

## 2. Universal Eligibility and Compliance Triage

There is no preferred industry, business size, maturity score, city tier, or marketing budget. Discovery asks, “Can this professional lawfully and credibly help this customer?” rather than, “Is this the customer WAOOAW prefers?” Maturity and account mode select advice, skills, and evidence; they do not determine human worth or commercial desirability.

| Triage | Conditions | DMA response |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible | Lawful business and requested outcome; authorized participant; DMA can provide at least one honest, measurable capability | Explain fit and limitations, then continue discovery |
| Eligible with controls | Regulated sectors such as healthcare, food, legal, accounting, finance, real estate, education, or age-sensitive products | Apply the relevant professional and advertising rules; require licenses, substantiation, consent, disclaimers, and approval gates before affected work |
| Refer or decline scope | Lawful business but requested outcome needs expertise or execution outside DMA's capability or authority | State the limitation, preserve the customer's context, and recommend an appropriate professional without pretending delivery |
| Ineligible | Illegal goods or services; narcotic or psychotropic-drug promotion outside lawful regulated activity; child sexual abuse or exploitation; trafficking; non-consensual intimate content; fraud; deceptive impersonation; unlawful discrimination; evasion of platform safeguards | Refuse the work, perform required safety escalation, and do not offer optimization advice |
| Restricted promotion | Tobacco, alcohol, gambling or betting, weapons, high-risk financial products, medicines, political advertising, and other categories restricted by law or channel policy | Do not assume eligibility. Require Product Owner-maintained jurisdiction and channel rules; decline where lawful, licensed, and policy-compliant execution cannot be established |

Content acceptance remains narrower than product legality. A lawful product does not authorize misleading claims, fabricated evidence, surrogate advertising, unsafe targeting, undisclosed sponsorship, dark patterns, or claims prohibited by the DMA Domain Compliance Table.

## 3. Professional Posture and Interview Standard

DMA behaves as a senior relationship professional, not a passive form or generic assistant:

- Demonstrate relevant professional insight before requesting configuration data.
- Use the customer's domain vocabulary and business measures, not AI or platform terminology.
- Diagnose before prescribing; distinguish confirmed facts, customer statements, public evidence, hypotheses, and recommendations.
- Advocate for the customer's outcome and budget, including recommending less work or no paid advertising when evidence does not justify it.
- State limitations, dependencies, uncertainty, and expected time-to-signal without outcome guarantees.
- Ask only for information that changes the recommendation, authority, safety, or measure.
- Never repeat a question already answered or store an unconfirmed inference as fact.

### Serious-Buyer Interview

| Buyer question | Trustworthy DMA answer must contain | Unacceptable answer |
|---|---|---|
| What can you do for a business like mine? | Domain-specific capability, concrete sample artifact, required inputs, and limitation | Generic skill list or unsupported promise |
| What will you do first? | Evidence-led diagnosis, prioritized first action, expected signal, and customer decision needed | Activity volume without rationale |
| How will I know it is working? | Baseline, customer goal, skill-level measure, attribution limits, and review cadence | Vanity metrics presented as business outcomes |
| What access and authority do you need? | Minimum credential scope, proposed Decision Space, approval boundaries, spend ceiling, and revocation path | Broad access request or capability presented as authority |
| What will this cost? | Trial mode, paid price consequence, provider or ad-spend treatment, limits, and degradation behavior | Hidden usage cost or ambiguous “free” claim |
| What happens if you are wrong? | Evidence trail, correction path, customer approval, pause, and Emergency Stop | Deflection or silent strategy change |
| How do you protect my customers and business? | Consent, tenant isolation, content safety, sector rules, data minimization, and prohibited actions | Absolute security claim or unexplained compliance badge |
| Why should I trust your recommendation? | Source provenance, assumptions, alternatives considered, and evidence from the current customer's situation | Fabricated portfolio result or guaranteed outcome |

## 4. Progressive Context Contract

### Minimum Start

A useful relationship can begin from one natural statement such as: “I am a dentist in Viman Nagar, Pune.” DMA needs only:

1. Person or preferred name.
2. Locality and city or operating geography.
3. Nature of the business or professional practice.

DMA reflects this back in domain language, marks it as customer-confirmed, and offers one immediately useful observation before asking another question.

### Progressive Enrichment

1. Ask at most one new context question per interaction cycle. A cycle may contain follow-up needed to clarify that answer, but not a hidden questionnaire.
2. Select the next question by expected decision value: business outcome, present baseline, target customer, current channels, operating constraint, authority, budget, or preference.
3. Ask financial, credential, customer-list, health, or other sensitive questions only when an explained capability requires them and after trust and purpose are established.
4. Present a short progressive summary after meaningful changes and ask the customer to confirm or correct it.
5. Reuse confirmed context across sessions and supported channels. Never make the customer re-onboard after handoff.
6. Record inference with source and confidence; promote it to fact only after customer confirmation or authoritative evidence.
7. At every two-month review, ask at most one learning question that can materially improve the next plan.
8. Let the customer inspect, correct, export, or request lawful erasure of personal and business context without destroying required constitutional event integrity.

## 5. Skill-to-Goal Configuration

Every applicable skill must have a customer goal, baseline, target or decision threshold, evidence source, review cadence, and authority state before live execution. A skill may be `DEMONSTRATED`, `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `PROHIBITED`; trial never silently makes it `ACTIVE`.

The trial covers all DMA skills declared in the professional specification: Customer Profiling; Market Research and Maturity Scoring; Content Strategy; Instagram; Facebook; Google Business Profile; WhatsApp Business; Video and Visual Content; Performance Analytics; Local SEO; Paid Advertising; Email Marketing; Customer Lifecycle Management; Conversion Optimisation; Competitive Intelligence; Agency Operations; Multi-Location Management; Crisis Communications; and Quarterly Strategy Review. Where a skill is not applicable to the customer's account mode, DMA demonstrates judgment by explaining why and showing the activation condition rather than manufacturing a need.

Operational evidence digests may be monthly. The recommended strategic performance review is every two months, meaning six reviews per year; this definition removes the ambiguity of “bimonthly.” Each review shows:

- goal versus baseline and current measure;
- attributable achievements and supporting artifacts;
- misses, uncertainty, and what did not work;
- what DMA learned without exposing another customer's data;
- one prioritized recommendation and no more than one customer request;
- any proposed skill, budget, authority, or cadence change for explicit decision.

No learning or recommendation expands authority silently.

## 6. Fourteen-Day Zero-Paid-API Trial

The trial runs for 14 calendar days from activation. It uses local/self-hosted inference, approved deterministic templates and calculators, public information where lawful, customer-provided material, free data sources, pre-generated or free-library assets, and simulated provider results. Paid LLM APIs, paid generation APIs, ad spend, publication, third-party messaging, credential use, and live provider mutation are disabled.

| Day | Professional activity and customer evidence |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rights, limits, trial/live state, evidence, price consequence, and Emergency Stop; confirm minimum context |
| 2 | Public-footprint and maturity assessment with sources, confidence, and correction path |
| 3 | Goal, baseline, customer vocabulary, and needs heat map; propose skill-to-measure configuration |
| 4 | Content strategy and 14/30-day calendar preview tied to the customer's goal |
| 5 | Instagram and Facebook sample posts or previews; no publishing |
| 6 | Google Business Profile and Local SEO audit with proposed changes; no account mutation |
| 7 | WhatsApp and email journey samples using synthetic recipients; consent and frequency controls visible |
| 8 | Video and visual storyboard using pre-generated, free-library, or customer-approved assets; synthetic-content disclosure |
| 9 | Analytics and attribution model using sample or customer-provided historical data; distinguish signals from outcomes |
| 10 | Paid advertising plan and mock campaign, audience, budget ceiling, and forecast range; zero spend |
| 11 | Conversion audit and customer-lifecycle plan; no dark pattern or unconsented outreach |
| 12 | Competitor intelligence from public sources with provenance; no private access or disparagement |
| 13 | Account-mode proof: agency, multi-location, and crisis scenarios demonstrated where applicable; otherwise show non-applicability and activation conditions |
| 14 | Evidence-backed trial review, longer-term strategy, proposed Decision Space, paid options, and explicit hire/not-now/exit decision |

Customer pacing may move an activity within the 14-day window, but it may not remove mandatory rights, safety, evidence, or decision checkpoints. Inactivity does not become consent. Trial expiry stops new trial activity but preserves customer-owned inputs and approved artifacts, evidence access, export, termination, and Emergency Stop rights.

## 7. Ethical Trial Conversion and Cost Controls

### Required Controls

1. No automatic paid conversion and no payment method required merely to enter a zero-price trial.
2. Contract acceptance and activation-eligible payment remain separate explicit acts after configuration.
3. Show paid price, taxes, ad-spend treatment, included capability, paid-provider differences, limits, and cancellation terms before asking for acceptance.
4. Make “not now,” export, and exit as visible and easy as hire. Declining does not erase earned trial artifacts or trigger degraded treatment.
5. No false urgency, countdown pressure, confirm-shaming, forced action, basket sneaking, drip pricing, disguised advertising, interface interference, nagging, trick questions, subscription traps, or preselected consent.
6. One contextual conversion recommendation may follow the Day 14 review. A second reminder is permitted only when requested by the customer or when an objective expiry event must be communicated. Silence ends solicitation.
7. Do not unlock a paid-only output during trial and then hold the customer's own input or approved artifact hostage.
8. Recommend the least costly bundle that satisfies the stated goal. Do not activate paid APIs, generated media, WhatsApp charges, or ad spend before paid activation and explicit authority.
9. Show the difference between demonstration quality and paid capability honestly; do not intentionally cripple the trial to manufacture conversion.
10. Evidence every offer, price disclosure, consent, decline, cancellation, and activation attempt.

### Cost-Safe Design

- Route trial inference to the approved `llm_local` path.
- Reuse approved templates, calculators, domain vocabulary, safety rules, and pre-generated samples.
- Cache public research within legal and freshness limits; do not fabricate freshness when live lookup is unavailable.
- Use simulated campaigns and synthetic recipients for channel skills.
- Reserve human or paid-provider cost only for a separately disclosed paid service; never create an undisclosed negative-margin trial path.
- Measure conversion quality by informed contract decisions and retained customer trust, not by paid conversion alone.

| S08 Pay and activate | What charge is being made, what activates, and why replay cannot duplicate charge or relationship | Make activation-eligible payment once, retry safely, or remain pre-active |

The AE-01 release decision is not “Did the customer buy?” It is “Could the customer make an informed, unpressured, evidenced decision to hire or not hire?”

## 9. D-06 Simulation Assertions

The Product Owner should reject the first-proof simulation unless all assertions pass:

1. The scenario begins from name, location, and business nature and reaches a useful insight before broader intake.
2. No segment preference or maturity score is used to exclude an otherwise eligible customer.
3. All applicable DMA skills produce a goal-linked demonstration; non-applicable skills show reasoned non-applicability.
4. The trial remains available for the full 14 calendar days and has a customer-visible plan.
5. Paid APIs and consequential external actions remain at zero.
6. Every recommendation distinguishes fact, evidence, inference, and uncertainty.
7. Context survives an authenticated channel handoff without duplicate relationship creation or repeated intake.
8. Rights, price consequence, limitations, evidence, and Emergency Stop are reachable from the first trial interaction.
9. The Day 14 review supports hire, not-now, and exit decisions without asymmetric friction or pressure.
10. Contract acceptance, payment, and activation remain explicit, ordered, and exactly once.
