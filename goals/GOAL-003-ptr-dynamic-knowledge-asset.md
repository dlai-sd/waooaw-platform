## Goal Statement

Redesign the Platform Type Registry (PTR) from a static pre-sprint script into a **dynamic, Goal-scoped, multi-stack Constitutional Knowledge Asset** assembled and maintained by the Goal Orchestrator throughout Goal execution. Establish the **Canonical Pattern Library** as the durable institutional memory that grows from Goal Learning Records. Ensure MagicLLM always operates on accurate, complete, scoped knowledge — never on stale snapshots or manual artifacts.
