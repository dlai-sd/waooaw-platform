## Enterprise Boundary Decision

The Founder Commercial Governance program is a federated institutional decision capability, not
a new system of record and not a new operational platform. Its enterprise purpose is to compose
version-pinned owner facts and governed projections into an evidenced offerability disposition,
then preserve the disposition, policy basis, assumptions, validity, and evidence references
without taking ownership of the facts it considered.

WC-065 is bounded to the decision required before publication or hiring. It may assemble the
approved professional version, declared skill composition, customer-goal envelope, resource and
provider feasibility, advertising envelope, commercial facts, policy, projections, assumptions,
and customer impact. It may produce one current disposition from `ALLOW`,
`ALLOW_CALCULATED_RISK`, `REVISE`, `ESCALATE`, or `BLOCK`. It may not convert that composition
into a second lifecycle, financial, execution, provider, authorization, or evidence truth.

The program boundary begins when an eligible offering composition requires a commercial and
constitutional offerability decision. It ends when the current disposition and its evidence
reference are available to the publication or hiring guard, or when an explicit unresolved or
failed state prevents further action. Active-employment oversight, settled reconciliation,
portfolio economics, institutional learning, and helpdesk administration remain outside WC-065.

### Institutional Actor Boundaries

- The Founder owns umbrella-policy approval, numeric commercial policy values, consequential
  exceptions outside delegated authority, and later implementation confirmation. Founder access
  does not create authority to override constitutional or protected commercial floors.
- The customer owns the decision to hire or continue under disclosed terms and retains notice,
  review, choice, applicable grandfathering, and remedy. An internal offerability disposition is
  not customer consent.
- The constitutional professional remains governed by its lifecycle-approved version, declared
  skills, Decision Space, and limitations. Commercial composition may reference these facts but
  may not amend them.
- The commercial-governance program owns the version-pinned proposal, scenario comparison,
  disposition, validity, assumptions, and owner evidence references needed to reconstruct its
  decision. Those are program records, not copies of each owner's operational truth.

## Duplication Controls

1. **Reference owner truth; do not reproduce it.** BP and the Founder View may retain identifiers,
   versions, projections, decisions, and evidence references needed for reconstruction. They must
   not maintain an independently mutable copy of WBE, lifecycle, PR, AIR, CTG, CE, or provider
   truth.
2. **Preserve semantic categories.** Expected, provisional, and settled cost; customer price;
   billed and earned revenue; markup; contribution and fully loaded planning margin; tax; cash;
   receivables; refunds; and credits remain distinct. A shared display does not merge ownership.
3. **Do not recompute owner decisions in orchestration.** BP may coordinate and compare approved
   results but may not recreate WBE financial validation, lifecycle eligibility, AIR selection,
   CTG authorization, or CE permission logic.
4. **Do not promote caches to truth.** A cached owner value remains a versioned reference with
   freshness and invalidation status. When validity cannot be established, publication and hiring
   fail closed.
5. **One disposition per assessed version.** Concurrent or superseding policy, offering,
   professional, skill, goal, or resource versions invalidate reuse of an older disposition;
   they do not create parallel current truths.
6. **Evidence pointers are not evidence copies.** Program records may cite durable evidence but
   may not create a mutable shadow ledger or treat absence of a pointer as permission.
7. **Learning remains propositional.** Commercial and portfolio observations may be routed to
   policy or agent lifecycle owners. They never become direct edits to governed artifacts.
8. **Configuration extends categories, not ownership.** New provider, resource, charging-unit,
   cost, or professional types must enter through their owning governance process and preserve the
   same boundaries; extension must not require a new source of truth in Founder View.

## Resilience And Fail-Closed Principles

| Condition | Required enterprise behavior |
|---|---|
| Owner unavailable | Keep the owner state explicitly unavailable; do not substitute a local estimate for an authoritative fact; block publication or hiring when the missing fact is required |
| Owner data stale, expired, or superseded | Invalidate the affected scenario and any reusable disposition; require refreshed owner evidence before reconsideration |
| Owner facts contradictory or disputed | Preserve each attributed state and route the conflict to the owning Decision Space; do not choose an optimistic value in orchestration |
| Projection lacks confidence or provenance | Treat it as insufficient for the applicable policy unless the policy owner has explicitly permitted that bounded evidence class; never present it as settled truth |
| Policy missing, expired, or changed concurrently | No delegated offerability permission exists; require current policy and renewed assessment or confirmation |
| Lifecycle eligibility missing or changed | Block publication and hiring; commercial governance cannot grant or restore professional eligibility |
| WBE simulation or financial validation unavailable | Keep financial outcome unresolved and fail closed; BP cannot recompute or infer a margin position |
| AIR, PR, CTG, or provider feasibility unavailable | Preserve an explicit feasibility unknown; block where the customer promise depends on that capability or resource |
| CE unavailable or evidence write fails | Return no successful or reusable disposition and perform no publication or hiring action that depends on it |
| Partial workflow completion | Resume only from owner-confirmed, idempotent evidence; do not infer downstream success from an earlier step or replay an external side effect blindly |
| Concurrent offer or policy change | Reject the stale comparison or confirmation; refresh all impacted versions and customer consequences before a new decision |
| Cross-tenant or insufficiently assured request | Deny without revealing the existence, composition, or economics of another customer or offering |

Resilience means preserving constitutional and customer safety under degradation, not maximizing
availability of an `ALLOW` result. `REVISE`, `ESCALATE`, `BLOCK`, and explicit unresolved states
are valid operational outcomes. Silent fallback, optimistic defaults, stale authorization reuse,
and success before evidence are prohibited architecture.

The dependency chain follows Emit-Transport-Listen, step dependency ordering, and idempotency at
the enterprise level: each consequential stage emits an attributable result; the next stage
consumes only an eligible result; `FAIL`, `HALT`, `ERROR`, invalid, or unresolved status prevents
forward movement; and external side effects require prior-success detection before retry.

### Proof Of WC-065 Independent Safety

WC-065 is independently safe only when its own approved package can:

- identify every authoritative owner and distinguish facts, projections, assumptions, policy,
  dispositions, and evidence references;
- block publication and hiring on stale, unavailable, contradictory, disputed, superseded,
  expired, ineligible, or unevidenced inputs;
- preserve constitutional and commercial floors without a later reconciliation or oversight
  iteration correcting the decision;
- show customer impact, notice, choice, and applicable continuity treatment before a prospective
  change is relied upon;
- invalidate stale previews and stale dispositions after any material version conflict;
- record no success before durable evidence confirmation; and
- route learning and unresolved owner matters without mutating financial, lifecycle, execution,
  provider, authorization, or evidence truth.

If any of these behaviors is deferred to WC-066, WC-067, WC-068, or WC-069, the proposed WC-065
package is architecturally incomplete and must not be implementation-authorized.
