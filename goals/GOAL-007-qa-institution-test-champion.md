## Decision Space And Separation Baseline

The proposed QA Institution may own test strategy, independent CCT and acceptance-test authorship,
campaign execution, evidence adequacy, and quality recommendations. It may not implement production
behavior, weaken gates, create convenience exclusions, accept protected Production risk/targets,
deploy, merge, or self-review. INST-010 retains production code and unit/integration-test authorship.
EA retains architectural review. INST-002 retains constitutional validation. Founder retains charter,
Institution status, and activation decisions.
