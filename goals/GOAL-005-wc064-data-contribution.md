## Data Architecture Decision

WC-065 requires one reconstructable, version-pinned offerability decision history. The Business
Platform owns that program history because it owns public offering orchestration and publication
or hiring enforcement. The history composes references to owner facts and owner-qualified
projections; it does not absorb their ownership or become a financial, lifecycle, execution,
provider, authorization, or constitutional ledger.

WBE remains authoritative for every financial fact and financial simulation, including price,
included budgets, usage, payment, tax, collection, refunds, credits, cost attribution, margin
validation, and reconciliation state. Agent lifecycle remains authoritative for professional,
version, skill, Decision Space, approval, and eligibility facts. PR and AIR remain authoritative
for their respective feasibility and expected-use projections. CTG and provider owners remain
authoritative for governed external-call and provider facts. CE remains authoritative for
constitutional authorization and immutable constitutional evidence.

The WC-065 program record may retain the minimum version-pinned composition, assumptions,
scenario comparison, preview, confirmation, disposition, validity, customer impact, invalidation
lineage, and evidence references needed to explain its own decision. It must not retain a mutable
shadow copy of an owner's operational or financial record.

### Effective Dating And Immutable History

Every versioned policy, offering, professional, skill, goal envelope, resource envelope, price,
cost basis, projection, scenario, preview, confirmation, and disposition distinguishes:

- when the owner produced or recorded the meaning;
- when the underlying event or observation occurred;
- when the meaning becomes effective for its governed purpose;
- when it ceases to be valid, expires, or is superseded;
- when the composition observed or last confirmed it; and
- which predecessor, successor, correction, dispute, or invalidation relationship applies.

Recorded time never substitutes for effective time. Effective time never proves when a fact was
observed. A prospective price or policy may coexist with the currently effective version without
overwriting it. A new owner version, correction, dispute, expiry, reconciliation, or decision
creates a successor fact and lineage. Historical scenarios, confirmations, dispositions, and
evidence references are never edited to look as though they used the successor meaning.

There is at most one disposition eligible for current reuse for the exact assessed version and
purpose. Older dispositions remain historical. A concurrent material change invalidates the
current preview and any uncommitted confirmation; a change after disposition creates an explicit
invalidation or supersession event and requires a new assessment before publication or hiring.

## Attribution And Reconciliation Model

### Attribution

Every scenario ingredient and financial meaning identifies:

- accountable source owner and source-relative subject;
- tenant, offering, professional, skill, goal, resource, customer-purpose, and period bindings
  applicable to that meaning;
- source and semantic-contract versions;
- charging or measurement unit and currency treatment where applicable;
- direct attribution or the versioned allocation policy used for shared attribution;
- production, observation, effective, validity, and last-confirmation meanings;
- expected, provisional, or settled status;
- assumptions, confidence, uncertainty, and unavailable behavior for projections; and
- correction, dispute, supersession, and evidence lineage.

Attribution does not transfer ownership. Shared costs require a governed allocation basis and may
support fully loaded planning margin, but they cannot be inserted as invented direct costs to
repair a scenario. Pass-through funds and provider credits remain bound to their customer purpose
and cannot be pooled across customers. A reusable capability or charging category does not make
another professional's observed cost, trust, eligibility, or prior disposition portable.

### Reconciliation

Reconciliation is an owner process that compares meanings without collapsing them:

1. WBE reconciles expected cost to provisional cost and provisional cost to settled cost using
   source events, provider statements, FX treatment, allocations, corrections, and the declared
   reconciliation scope.
2. WBE separately reconciles billed consideration, earned revenue, receivables, cash, tax,
   refunds, credits, and pass-through amounts. Equality between any two is never presumed.
3. BP reconciles its retained owner references and dependency versions with current owner
   outcomes. It may invalidate a scenario or disposition but may not settle or correct WBE truth.
4. Provider, AIR, PR, lifecycle, policy, and CE conflicts retain both attributed positions until
   the owner of the disputed meaning supplies a correction or resolution.
5. A later settled result may assess forecast quality and inform learning, but it does not rewrite
   the original expected scenario or retroactively validate publication or hiring.
6. A mismatch, missing owner, unsupported contract version, stale value, disputed meaning, or
   evidence failure remains explicit and blocks consequential reuse where required.

Reconciliation outcomes preserve their scope. A customer-period settlement does not imply that a
provider-period statement, tax position, revenue-recognition period, offering scenario, or
portfolio allocation is also settled.

## Evidence Relationships

Constitutional evidence and commercial decision history are related but not merged. CE records
the immutable constitutional event. BP retains the program decision and the authority-owned
evidence reference needed to demonstrate that authorization and recording occurred. WBE and other
owners retain their own financial and operational evidence under their approved ledgers.

The program distinguishes:

| Evidence state | Data meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | An evidence obligation or recording attempt exists, but the authority has not confirmed a durable record |
| Recorded | The authority confirmed durable evidence and supplied a valid subject-bound reference |
| Unavailable | The evidence authority, compatible contract, permitted reference, or record outcome cannot be obtained |
| Disputed | An authorized challenge to evidence accuracy, completeness, attribution, or sufficiency remains unresolved |
| Superseded | A later interpretation or linked event replaces the earlier meaning for current use while preserving the original record |

Transport acceptance, local persistence, confirmation intent, command dispatch, or an optimistic
projection is not recorded evidence. Evidence failure yields no successful or reusable
disposition. Correction, dispute, withdrawal, expiry, and erasure add lineage; they do not modify
or delete constitutional evidence. Sensitive or erasable payload remains outside immutable proof
whenever a non-reconstructive reference, hash, category, and subject binding are sufficient.

## Extensible Cost, Resource, And Charging Semantics

Extensibility is governed configuration inside existing ownership, not a new truth owner. A new
category is usable only when its owner, stable meaning, version, status, effective period,
measurement basis, charging unit, currency treatment, attribution rules, reconciliation method,
retention class, and evidence obligation are approved and available.

The cost taxonomy supports, without being limited to, direct, variable, fixed, shared,
pass-through, usage-based, contingent, tax-related, commitment, and Founder-defined categories.
Categories may be composed, so a cost may be both variable and shared or both direct and
usage-based. Composition must be explicit; no category name implies another dimension.

Charging semantics support at least these independent unit classes:

| Unit class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Provider charging unit | The quantity on which an external provider prices or reports usage |
| Internal resource unit | The operational quantity WAOOAW measures for capacity, attribution, or procurement |
| Customer UsageUnit | The customer-understandable service entitlement or consumption meaning required by C-051 |
| Financial unit | Currency and precision governed by the financial owner |
| Time or commitment unit | A period, reservation, minimum commitment, or capacity window |
| Outcome-linked unit | A governed business quantity used only where its attribution and commercial meaning are explicitly approved |
| Founder-defined unit | A later governed unit whose semantics satisfy the same ownership, versioning, attribution, and reconciliation obligations |

Tokens are one possible technical ingredient, never the universal charging model. Conversion
between unit classes is a versioned owner rule with direction, validity, assumptions, precision,
and rounding meaning. No conversion is inferred from equal labels or historical ratios. Adding a
provider, resource, cost category, or charging unit preserves WBE financial authority, AIR or PR
feasibility ownership, CTG mediation, CE evidence, and BP's reference-only composition boundary.

## Retention And Minimisation

| Data class | Retention decision |
|---|---|
| Constitutional evidence | Append-only under constitutional retention; correction, dispute, or erasure adds proof and never deletes prior proof |
| Program decision history | Retain for the governed offering, publication/hiring, active commitment, renewal, dispute, reconciliation, and audit purposes; archive or dispose only under an approved retention policy that preserves required evidence references and immutable lineage |
| Owner fact and projection material | Retain source references and only the minimum exact owner-authored values needed for reconstruction; inherit owner validity and purpose limits and never become independently current after owner invalidation |
| Assumptions and calculated-risk basis | Retain with the decision they supported through its review, expiry, dispute, learning, and audit purpose; never detach and reuse as owner facts |
| Sensitive or customer payload | Exclude unless necessary and authorized; retain for the shortest approved purpose and preserve only non-reconstructive proof where immutable evidence is required |
| Preview and confirmation | Retain when consequential to a decision, conflict, abandonment, or dispute; otherwise dispose under the approved short-lived workflow policy without erasing a resulting decision or evidence relationship |
| Reconciliation and correction lineage | Retain sufficiently to connect original, provisional, settled, corrected, disputed, refunded, credited, and superseding meanings without rewriting any prior state |

Exact retention periods require the separately owned privacy, legal, operational, and
constitutional decisions. Their absence does not permit indefinite retention of payload or early
deletion of immutable proof.

## Migration Decision

**Decision: ADDITIVE MIGRATION REQUIRED for WC-065.**

The approved architecture has authoritative owner stores and constitutional evidence, but it does
not define an existing owner for the complete WC-065 program history of version-pinned scenarios,
previews, confirmations, dispositions, invalidations, and evidence relationships. WC-065 cannot
satisfy reconstructability, stale-conflict invalidation, immutable decision history, or
publication/hiring reuse checks through transient orchestration alone.

The required design-depth migration is one additive BP-owned persistence evolution for the
minimum retained decision record above. It must:

- add new versioned program records and relationships without overwriting historical business or
  constitutional records;
- preserve tenant isolation, owner attribution, effective dating, idempotency, correction,
  supersession, invalidation, and reconciliation lineage;
- store owner references and minimum reconstruction values rather than duplicate WBE, lifecycle,
  PR, AIR, CTG, provider, or CE truth;
- use additive evolution for any constitutional evidence relationship and perform no destructive
  operation on a constitutional ledger;
- require no movement, normalization, or relabelling of existing WBE financial truth;
- backfill no fabricated decision, confirmation, evidence, settled state, or current permission
  for historical offerings; and
- classify pre-existing offerings without a qualifying decision as requiring fresh assessment,
  not as implicitly allowed or migrated into success.

This decision does not name a persistence schema, table, column, index, migration tool command,
wire field, or implementation file. Whether existing implementation artifacts can satisfy parts
of the design is reserved for INST-010's read-only reality contribution; reuse may reduce the
eventual change set but may not remove the need for an authoritative retained WC-065 decision
history unless the integrated package demonstrates an already approved semantically equivalent
owner record.
