## Customer Outcome

A prospect can move from discovering a suitable WAOOAW professional to one active employment relationship through an informed, conversational, evidence-backed journey. Before commitment, the customer understands suitability, skills, limitations, rights, authority, cost, and expected outcomes without needing technical AI vocabulary.

## Generic Actors

| Actor | Need |
|---|---|
| Prospect / Evaluator | Determine suitability before commitment |
| Authorized Customer / Employer | Define outcome, budget, authority, rights, and contract boundaries |
| Business Outcome Owner | Define measurable KPIs and assess expected value |
| Delegated Relationship Manager | Operate only within employer-granted authority |
| Candidate Digital Professional | Demonstrate domain suitability, evidence, skills, and limitations |
| Low-technical-literacy customer | Complete the journey in occupational and business language |
| Risk-sensitive customer | Inspect rights, financial limits, evidence, and stop controls |
| Channel-constrained customer | Continue the same relationship through supported channels |

## Business Vocabulary

| Term | Confirmed meaning |
|---|---|
| Professional | Domain-specialized autonomous digital worker employed toward declared business outcomes |
| Capability | Work the professional can perform; capability does not confer authority |
| Trust | Confidence continuously earned through observable evidence |
| Authority | Licensed permission to act inside the agreed Decision Space |
| Trial / demonstration | Disclosed evaluation of proposed behavior, not AE-02 execution |
| Employment Contract | Accepted outcomes, skills, authority, rights, price, review, and lifecycle terms |
| Evidence | Observable and attributable proof of proposals, decisions, acknowledgements, and outcomes |
| Emergency Stop | Customer's unconditional right to halt professional activity |

## Capability Boundaries and Proof

- AE-01 ends at one active professional relationship; real professional work belongs to AE-02.
- Trial changes no external state: no publishing, spending, third-party messaging, or trading.
- Capabilities remain generic; DMA supplies first-proof vocabulary and examples only.
- Channel changes do not create another relationship or alter contract terms.
- D-01 defines business obligations, not architecture, APIs, schemas, code, or tests.
- The first proof is a WhatsApp-first DMA simulation traversing EC-01 through EC-10 with zero undisclosed rights, limits, charges, or executed trial actions.
