The validation question is whether the Product contribution defines customer business outcomes that can be judged honestly through a baseline, measure, evidence, review period, attribution boundary, uncertainty, and customer action without substituting work completion or technical performance for customer value.

The answer is **yes, with the conditions in this record**. The contribution is compatible with the generic F4 meanings of Plan, Work, Results, Usage & budget, Rights & control, truthful state, authoritative attention, and measurable acceptance. It preserves the generic distinctions that:

1. a plan is not completed work;
2. completed work is not a business outcome;
3. a technical or ad-platform metric is not customer value;
4. a forecast is not an actual;
5. evidence supports a result claim but does not manufacture success; and
6. an observed business change is not attributable to DMA merely because DMA work occurred in the same period.

## 3. Baseline And Measure Fitness

The baseline semantics are **FIT** for F4 business use because they require the starting outcome meaning, customer vocabulary, observation period, material context or seasonality, source and confirmation basis, completeness, included and excluded channels/campaigns/locations/services/cohorts/event states, qualification and deduplication rules, cancellation and no-show treatment, attribution rule, and strategy-change boundary.

The following conditions are mandatory:

- missing history is **Baseline needed**, never zero;
- a customer estimate remains **customer-stated baseline** until authoritative confirmation;
- a benchmark is context, not the customer's baseline;
- a target is not a baseline;
- a material change to qualification, booking process, campaign objective, audience, offer, operating capacity, channel connection, evidence method, or review context requires a compatibility review and may require a new baseline or bounded observation period; and
- no baseline may be compared with a measure whose event meaning, cohort, period, exclusions, or evidence status is incompatible.

The measure semantics are **FIT** because every selected measure must name its customer-language label, counted event, unit, period, cohort, inclusions, exclusions, source, evidence status, attribution rule, validity, and accountable owner. Quantitative and bounded qualitative assessments are acceptable only when their method, evidence, uncertainty, and limitation are explicit.

This validation creates **no numeric target, benchmark, threshold, conversion promise, attribution window, price, fee, allowance, budget consequence, commercial policy, or customer segment**. A target or decision threshold must come from the customer's separately governed goal configuration. Existing specification examples or capability-map numbers cannot be reused as observed results or default policy.

## 4. Evidence-Source Fitness

The evidence-source package is **FIT WITH SOURCE QUALIFICATION**:

| Source family | Valid business use | Business limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Authoritative customer booking, CRM, scheduling, order, enrolment, or equivalent records | Confirm eligible visits, cancellations, no-shows, repeat outcomes, cohorts, and offline facts | Ownership, event meaning, consent, completeness, duplicates, freshness, and relationship binding must be known |
| Approved booking or domain-equivalent projection | Confirm a visit outcome and source where the authoritative owner supplies both | An attempt or accepted request is not confirmation; integration and attribution may be unavailable |
| Connected analytics and disciplined campaign tags | Support direct, first-touch, last-touch, or journey correlation | Tagging, consent, event configuration, connected sources, compatible periods, and unobserved paths limit the claim |
| Channel and ad-platform analytics | Support touchpoint and diagnostic evidence | Provider-reported reach, impression, click, engagement, lead, or modelled conversion is not automatically a qualified enquiry, booking, revenue event, or causal outcome |
| Authoritative actual customer ad spend | Support spend-efficiency assessment | Must match the outcome scope and period; budget, forecast, fee, provider cost, and allowance are different meanings |
| Customer-confirmed statement or record | Supply a provisional baseline, offline result, correction, or source context | Must be labelled customer-stated or customer-confirmed, not independently observed |
| Public market or competitor evidence | Explain market, seasonality, constraints, and strategic context | Does not prove this customer's acquisition, conversion, retention, revenue, or spend result |
| Constitutional evidence, professional work records, and provider receipts | Prove a proposal, decision, authority event, limitation disclosure, action, or execution fact | Constitutional compliance, work completion, transport success, and provider health do not prove the intended business outcome |

`PENDING`, `RECORDED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `DISPUTED`, and `SUPERSEDED` remain distinct. A command receipt, local state, correlation identifier, recording attempt, provider response, or execution receipt cannot be presented as recorded outcome evidence.

## 5. Attribution, Uncertainty, And Truthfulness

The attribution semantics are **FIT WITH A NON-CAUSALITY CONDITION**. Direct source attribution, tagged journey attribution, explicitly named first-touch or last-touch attribution, bounded multi-touch correlation, and labelled customer-confirmed attribution are valid bases when evidence supports them. None may be silently upgraded to exclusive causation.

Every result must disclose material limits from missing tags or sources, disconnected analytics or business records, offline and cross-device journeys, platform self-reporting, modelled conversions, duplicates, spam, cancellations, no-shows, changing qualification rules, delayed outcomes, incomplete or small cohorts, stale evidence, consent restrictions, and material influences outside DMA control such as customer operations, availability, price, reputation, service quality, seasonality, competitors, or economic conditions.

The strongest truthful statement may be that DMA **contributed to**, **was associated with**, or **cannot currently be linked to** an outcome. Use **Attribution unknown** when no supported link can be established. It must not be replaced by organic, zero, unchanged, failure, success, or no impact. Use **Outcome unavailable** when a compatible authoritative outcome source is absent or unsupported, **Evidence pending** while recording or reconciliation is incomplete, **Stale** after validity or a material change, **Disputed** when an authorized party challenges the meaning, **Baseline needed** when no fit comparison exists, and **Review period open** before a valid assessment point.

Separately authoritative facts may coexist without being collapsed: work may be completed, actual spend may be known, confirmed visits may be known, and DMA attribution may still be unknown.

## 6. Cadence, Attention, And Customer Action

The review cadence is **FIT**:

- monthly operational digests may explain what happened, what was learned, what was tried, what changes next, and at most one customer decision request;
- the strategic performance review occurs **every two months**, meaning six reviews per year, and assesses the customer goal against its compatible baseline and current measure, including achievements, misses, uncertainty, learning, and one prioritized recommendation;
- a material exception may require earlier review without changing the strategic cadence or silently resetting the baseline; and
- the customer may request review of evidence, attribution, baseline, goal, constraint, or material change.

Freshness remains owner- and purpose-relative. Every assessment identifies its production time, observation period, last authoritative confirmation, declared validity, source version, and correction lineage. Browser refresh does not make evidence fresh, and absent validity does not mean indefinite validity.

The material attention candidates are **FIT WITH AUTHORITATIVE QUALIFICATION**. Baseline incompatibility, attribution uncertainty, unavailable or stale evidence, a material outcome miss, continuing customer-funded spend after a miss review, an owner-reported spend boundary, a disconnected dependency, a required customer approval or information item, a material legal/domain/consent constraint, and a material complaint or prohibited claim may be candidates because each carries a customer consequence and a valid response.

Permitted customer actions include, only where the authoritative relationship state makes them available:

- confirm or correct a baseline;
- inspect evidence or challenge attribution;
- acknowledge a limitation or defer a consequential decision;
- reconnect or provide an approved source;
- review the diagnosis and owner-approved options;
- request a governed goal or plan change;
- change pacing or pause affected work;
- approve or reject an exact subject;
- distinctly confirm a scope boundary where required;
- provide required information or decline; and
- take no action after seeing the known consequence where that option is owner-approved.

The professional or source owner may contribute a candidate, but the generic relationship authority alone qualifies it for public **Needs your attention** and supplies its order. No candidate carries a local rank. Informational content volume, delivery, engagement movement, provider telemetry, or technical alert does not qualify unless an accountable owner translates it into a material business consequence and valid customer action.

## 7. Headline Outcome Prohibition

The following may never become a headline DMA business outcome or establish customer value by themselves:

- latency, retries, queue state, execution time, provider success, delivery health, model use, token use, API use, request volume, or platform resource consumption;
- reach, impressions, plays, saves, clicks, click-through rate, follower movement, page views, form completion, handoff speed, or provider-modelled conversion;
- campaign setup, brief approval, creative approval, publication, content-calendar adherence, channel connection, message delivery, reminder delivery, review-request delivery, or segmentation; and
- specification completion, Activation Gate status, repository tests, constitutional evidence, campaign launch, professional work completion, deliverable completion, transport acceptance, or technical health.

These facts may diagnose operation, qualify evidence, show work, or explain uncertainty. They cannot occupy the business-outcome field, set achieved status, manufacture attribution, or excuse an unsupported success claim. Under C-037, technically excellent work that does not improve the customer's stated outcome is not business success. Under C-049, a material inability to deliver or measure the goal requires honest limitation disclosure and review rather than continued output production presented as progress.
