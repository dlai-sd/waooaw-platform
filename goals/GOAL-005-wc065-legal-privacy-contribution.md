## Decision Method And Legal Posture

The approved institutional policies identify DLAI Satellite Data (OPC) Pvt Ltd, India-facing
processing, DPDPA-based privacy treatment, customer notice and grievance routes, refund terms,
and purpose-specific retention assertions. Those policies are institutional source documents,
not proof by themselves that a statute is commenced, applies to each WC-065 processing operation,
or mandates each policy period.

The authoritative Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act 22 of 2023) is used only for
conditional constraints. Its section 1(2) makes commencement notification-dependent; section 3
defines application; section 4 limits processing to a lawful purpose on consent or specified
legitimate use; sections 5 and 6 govern notice and consent; section 8 governs Data Fiduciary
obligations and erasure when consent is withdrawn or the purpose is no longer served, subject to
retention necessary for legal compliance; sections 11 through 13 govern access information,
correction and erasure, and grievance redressal; and section 17 states exemptions. The supplied
WC-065 evidence does not establish the commencement and applicability facts, processing-purpose
map, affected Data Principal classes, consent/legitimate-use mapping, contracts, recipient roles,
or record taxonomy needed to apply those provisions conclusively. The official India Code page
also lists later rules and notifications; their commencement, transitional effect, and exact
application to WC-065 require qualified legal verification.

Accordingly, no topic below is converted from a constitutional, Product, Business, Data, or
Security constraint into a legal conclusion. `UNRESOLVED` means the Founder must direct qualified
external counsel and provide the named factual inputs before the integrated PDR-065-07 decision
can close.
