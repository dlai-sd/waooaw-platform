## Owner-Package Constitutional Sufficiency Assessment

For the limited purpose of the INST-002 owner contribution, the seven accepted contributions are
constitutionally sufficient to constrain reconciliation of the WC-064 design and detailed
grooming of WC-065. Together they provide attributable owner boundaries, Evidence First ordering,
non-waivable floors, honest uncertainty, prospective customer protections, proposal-only learning,
and explicit prohibited overrides. No owner contribution compensates for another Decision Space.

This is not a finding that the integrated package exists, is complete, is implementation-ready,
or has passed Constitutional readiness review. Sufficiency depends on reconciliation preserving
the matrix above, resolving or explicitly routing conflicts, retaining every protected decision
below as unresolved until its owner acts, and producing a version-pinned WC-065 package for the
separate fresh Enterprise Architecture and Constitutional readiness reviews.
