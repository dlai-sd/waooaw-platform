## 1. Contribution Decision

INST-004 publishes [ADR-046](../adr/ADR-046-workload-identity-and-service-authentication.md) as **PROPOSED**. The decision closes the Order 1 authoring obligation but does not close EA-F4-01 or accept the ADR. Acceptance requires later independent INST-003 and fresh INST-002 approval with every condition satisfied; INST-004 cannot approve its own work.

The selected architecture is one environment-parity model:

- mandatory mTLS for BP-to-WBE, BP-to-PR, and BP-to-approved-domain-adapter traffic in development, CI, and cloud;
- per-workload asymmetric certificates issued by an environment-scoped trust root;
- exact SPIFFE-style URI SAN workload identity, trust-domain, target-audience, route, operation, and contract-major policy;
- ephemeral local and per-run CI roots and short-lived leaf certificates generated by approved bootstrap/test harnesses, never shared secrets;
- environment-separated cloud private CA/certificates under Azure Key Vault custody and managed-identity delivery consistent with ADR-014;
- a short-lived BP-signed delegated actor/tenant/relationship/purpose/operation/version envelope, rebound by each target to authenticated BP and target-owned truth and never independently authoritative; and
- local authentication decisions that do not call CE, while preserving CE for constitutional validation, licensing, and Evidence First where the business operation requires it.

## 3. Preserved Ownership And Constitutional Boundaries

- BP remains the sole ordinary public F4 facade, relationship-governance projection owner, public command outcome owner, and privacy-safe error translator.
- WBE remains the only authority for allowance, billing actual, budget, ceiling, forecast, threshold, pacing, commercial consequence, and distinct `BLOCKED` outcomes.
- PR remains the internal professional-execution-truth owner and gains no public governance or relationship authority.
- CE remains the sole constitutional validation, authority-licensing, and constitutional-evidence authority. Authentication does not require a CE call and does not bypass later CE obligations.
- Professional/domain adapters remain private, explicitly registered semantic contributors. They cannot grant authority, own public ordering, calculate WBE truth, record constitutional evidence, or expose provider operations.
- Web continues to use only the BP public/generated-client boundary and receives no private host, service credential, delegated envelope, tenant authority header, ledger locator, or direct service route.
- Constitutional, customer-evidence, billing, usage, and provider ledgers remain private. This architecture creates no browser or workload-wide ledger access.

## 4. Accepted ADR Reconciliation

ADR-046 is grounded in accepted ADR-007's requirement that authorized internal CE traffic be mutually authenticated in cloud and in ADR-014's environment-separated secret custody and Key Vault model. It does not silently amend either decision.

ADR-007 remains controlling for BP-to-CE and PR-to-CE, including its currently accepted plain development rule. ADR-046 applies the stronger parity architecture only to its expressly authorized F4 routes. The disclosed mismatch is future ADR reconciliation work, not an implicit amendment. ADR-014 remains the custody decision; a `.env`, GitHub secret, database password, Keycloak client secret, API key, or HMAC value is not promoted into workload identity.

## 5. Explicit Prohibitions

The contribution specifically prohibits:

1. shared HMAC secrets as a development, CI, or other environment-specific substitute for workload identity;
2. caller-provided tenant, relationship, actor, role, purpose, operation, or version headers as authority;
3. plain HTTP or plaintext gRPC development/CI bypass;
4. generic certificate-CN, wildcard SAN, suffix, substring, or regular-expression matching without exact trust-domain identity, audience, route, and operation authorization;
5. CE calls as a prerequisite for TLS, workload, or delegated-envelope authentication;
6. developer password-manager or manual certificate ceremony as a trust root;
7. browser access to private services, providers, or ledgers;
8. authentication success being represented as business authorization, constitutional permission, evidence, or command success; and
9. policy-default, implementation, provider, deployment, F5-F8, or customer-proof claims from this architecture record.
