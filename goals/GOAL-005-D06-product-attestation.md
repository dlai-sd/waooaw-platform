## Product Decisions

1. AE-01 remains customer-agnostic. Any lawful customer for whom a supported professional can provide an honest measurable capability may evaluate that professional.
2. DMA is the first proof, not the generic product boundary.
3. Minimum context is name, location, and business nature. Context grows progressively, one decision-relevant question per interaction cycle, with confirmation, correction, preservation, and reuse.
4. DMA demonstrates all declared skills during trial through analysis, planning, previews, synthetic or customer-supplied material, and simulated execution. Trial performs no consequential external action and consumes no paid LLM or generation API.
5. Every proposed skill is linked to a customer goal, baseline, measure, evidence source, authority state, and review cadence.
6. The recommended strategic performance review occurs every two months. Monthly operational digests may supplement it.
7. Trial conversion is customer-initiated after explicit configuration and contract acceptance. No automatic paid conversion, payment method at trial entry, preselected consent, false urgency, nagging, hidden price, or loss of customer-owned trial artifacts is permitted.
8. The customer may decide `HIRE`, `NOT_NOW`, or `EXIT`; all three are valid informed outcomes.

## G5-TRIAL-POLICY-01 Amendment

The duration decision is amended from “14 calendar days from activation or 3 completed trial sessions, whichever occurs first” to:

> **14 calendar days from trial activation.** DMA maintains a customer-visible activity plan across the period. Session count does not shorten the entitlement. Customer inactivity does not become consent, extend authority, or trigger conversion.

All other accepted D-05 trial-policy fields remain unchanged. The one-credit-per-`tenant_id + professional_type` rule limits repeated free entitlement but does not select a preferred customer segment.

## D-06 Consumption Rules

- The ten AE-01 learning and decision outcomes in the domain synthesis are mandatory simulation checkpoints.
- The 14-day plan is a capability-coverage plan, not a requirement to pressure the customer into daily interaction.
- Non-applicable DMA skills must produce reasoned non-applicability and activation conditions rather than a manufactured need.
- Evidence must distinguish customer facts, public evidence, inference, recommendation, simulation, and actual external outcome.
- D-06 Work Contract candidates must reuse completed WC-031 through WC-043 foundations and must not re-specify them as greenfield work.
