## AE-01 Release Outcome

AE-01 proves that a prospect can discover, evaluate, interview, trial, configure, contract with, pay for, and hire a WAOOAW professional while understanding limits, cost, authority, rights, evidence, and Human Override. The first proof is a WhatsApp-first DMA simulation, while the release boundary remains generic.

## Release Boundary

**In scope:** AE-01-S01 through S10 as one continuous journey; informed disclosures; domain-aware interview; conversational context; disclosed trial; explicit configuration and contract acceptance; exactly-once payment activation; one authenticated channel handoff; evidence and Emergency Stop from first trial interaction.

**Out of scope:** real DMA campaign work; AE-02 through AE-06 detailed grooming; multi-agent and ecosystem behavior; architecture, APIs, schemas, security design, implementation tasks, sprint estimates, production authorization, or customer-proof claims based only on repository evidence.

## Product Gate Rules

1. D-01 through D-05 must close before D-06 finalizes release candidates.
2. PG-02, PG-03, PG-04, PG-07, PG-08, PG-10, and PG-11 require specification-level closure evidence.
3. Rights and control remain release-wide and precede commitment.
4. Discovery, interview, and minimum context precede trial.
5. Trial consequences precede configuration and conversion.
6. Configuration precedes contract; contract precedes payment and activation.
7. Durable relationship continuity is mandatory release proof.
8. No AE-01 story may be dropped because its shared gap is difficult.
9. Proposed Work Contract grouping occurs only in D-06.

## Simulation Acceptance Evidence

- One WhatsApp-first DMA scenario traverses all ten story checkpoints with `10/10 PASS`.
- Customer identifies trial/live state, price consequence, Decision Space, rights, and stop mechanism.
- No payment or activation precedes required disclosure and consent.
- Trial-to-Active conversion happens exactly once; replay creates no duplicate relationship or charge.
- One authenticated channel handoff preserves relationship and conversation context.
- Evidence and Emergency Stop are available from the first trial interaction.
- No foundation blocker or undisclosed degradation remains.

Simulation is D-06 readiness evidence, not post-implementation customer proof.

## Domain Authority Input

Sujay supplies DMA vocabulary, positioning, eligibility, interview scenarios, trial-safe capabilities, meaningful evidence, and trial-to-paid assumptions. INST-011 incorporates and attests that workshop evidence. Sujay does not define generic architecture, assign implementation work, or authorize release.

PCR-01 and BCG-01 are the same D-05 closure item.
