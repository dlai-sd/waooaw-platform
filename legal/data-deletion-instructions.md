# User Data Deletion Instructions

WAOOAW users may request deletion of personal data associated with their account, including data received through Google, Meta, Facebook, or WhatsApp integrations.

## Submit a deletion request

Email [customersupport@dlaisd.com](mailto:customersupport@dlaisd.com) with the subject **WAOOAW Data Deletion Request**.

Include:

- the email address or phone number associated with your WAOOAW account;
- the authentication provider you used, such as Google, Facebook, or WhatsApp;
- your Meta or Facebook user identifier when available; and
- a short description of the data or account you want deleted.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, one-time passwords, payment-card details, or government identification documents in the initial request.

## Verification and processing

WAOOAW will acknowledge the request and may ask for proportionate information needed to verify that the requester controls the affected account. After verification, WAOOAW will delete or irreversibly anonymize eligible personal data from active systems.

Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days. WAOOAW will confirm completion or explain any lawful reason that requires additional time.

## Retention exceptions

Some records may be retained when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security investigation, accounting, dispute resolution, or enforcement of constitutional governance obligations. Access to retained records remains restricted, and the records are removed when the applicable retention obligation expires.

Data contained in protected backups is isolated from normal processing and expires through the applicable backup-retention cycle.

## Questions

For questions about deletion or privacy rights, contact [customersupport@dlaisd.com](mailto:customersupport@dlaisd.com).
