Claim ID:        C-089
Type:            OBLIGATION
Statement:       The final customer-facing price for any WAOOAW product (agent bundle, top-up
                 plan, event pack, auto-refill) must exceed its bundle cost floor by at least
                 the Founder-set Constitutional Minimum Margin %. The bundle cost floor is
                 the sum of all marked-up thread costs at the declared ration for that product.
                 No agent, bundle, top-up, or promotional price may be set below this floor.
                 The WAOOAW Billing Engine (WBE) enforces this computationally: any pricing
                 configuration that produces a below-floor price is rejected at configuration
                 time, not at billing time. The Constitutional Minimum Margin % is set
                 exclusively by Founder Action and recorded in the Platform Procurement Ledger.
                 It may not be reduced by any other institution, office, or process.
Confidence:      100%
Source:          GOAL-004 brainstorming session 2026-07-30. Operational Scenario S-06 (margin
                 inversion): the Markup Engine derives bundle prices from ration costs. Without
                 a hard computational floor, a combination of zero thread markups and zero
                 platform margin could produce offerings that operate at cost — or below.
                 C-048 (Non-Exploitation) implicitly requires sustainable pricing: an agent
                 that operates below cost will be withdrawn, harming customers who depend on
                 it. C-089 makes this explicit and enforceable. It also protects against
                 accidental below-floor pricing during promotional pricing decisions or
                 mid-session markup adjustments where the composed effect is not obvious.
Depends On:      C-048 (Non-Exploitation — unsustainable pricing leads to service withdrawal,
                 which harms customers who depend on the agent), C-088 (Agent Billing Profile —
                 the Profile contains the cost floor derivation), C-091 (Thread Catalog
                 Sovereignty — floor is computed from Thread Catalog costs)
Produces:        WBE Markup Engine validation: reject_if(final_price < bundle_cost_floor *
                 (1 + min_margin_pct)); institutional.pricing_floor_log table (append-only
                 record of every pricing configuration check with outcome); Founder Action
                 template for margin floor adjustment; AGENT-ENTRY.md reflects current
                 Constitutional Minimum Margin % after each Founder Action update.
Contradicted By: NONE. Competitive pressure, market positioning, promotional pricing, or
                 agency discounts may not override the constitutional margin floor. The
                 Markup Engine may generate multiple pricing options for Founder review, but
                 it may not present or activate any option that is below floor.
Status:          RATIFIED — 2026-07-30 (Founder: Yogesh Khandge — GOAL-004 spec phase)
Reviewer Notes:  The Constitutional Minimum Margin % is intentionally left to Founder Action
                 rather than being fixed in the claim. Appropriate margin depends on platform
                 maturity, competitive context, and institutional growth stage. The claim
                 mandates enforcement of the floor, not the specific value of the floor.

                 Suggested initial value: 55%. This provides:
                 - Room for top-up discount (10% off event packs without breaching floor)
                 - Room for reseller/agency bulk discount (20% off without breaching floor
                   if bundle margin is 60%+)
                 - Buffer for unexpected cost increases (FX shift, provider price increase)
                   before a pricing review is required

                 The floor applies to ALL products: bundles, top-ups, event packs, auto-refill
                 charges. A top-up priced at cost (0% margin) to incentivize usage is a
                 C-089 violation, even if the bundle has 80% margin. Each product is
                 evaluated independently against the floor.
