Claim ID:        C-090
Type:            LAW
Statement:       A WAOOAW customer's subscription price may not increase during an active
                 contract period. Price changes — including bundle price increases resulting
                 from provider cost changes, FX movements, or platform margin adjustments —
                 apply only at the customer's next renewal. Every price change requires 30
                 calendar days advance notice to the affected customer before it takes effect.
                 This notice is a constitutional act: it must be recorded in the
                 Constitutional Audit Ledger with the customer's acknowledgment timestamp
                 before the new price is activated. A price change without a recorded
                 acknowledgment is constitutionally void — the old price continues until
                 acknowledgment is received. The customer's right to cancel at any time
                 (without penalty) upon receiving a price change notice is explicitly preserved.
                 This claim applies to all WAOOAW products: bundles, auto-refill authorizations,
                 standing top-up arrangements, and any recurring charge.
Confidence:      100%
Source:          GOAL-004 brainstorming session 2026-07-30. Operational Scenario S-17 (thread
                 pricing change impact on existing subscribers). Classification: LAW because
                 this reflects binding consumer protection principles under Indian Consumer
                 Protection Act 2019 and general contract law — a recurring subscription is
                 a contract; unilateral mid-contract price increases are voidable. WAOOAW's
                 constitutional commitment to non-exploitation (C-048) demands the same
                 protection even where law might technically permit variation clauses. The
                 claim is LAW because it is both legally grounded and constitutionally required.
Depends On:      C-048 (Non-Exploitation — price changes without notice exploit customer
                 lock-in), C-007 (Constitutional Audit Ledger — acknowledgment must be
                 append-only recorded), C-051 (Resource Transparency — customer must know

Produces:        business.price_change_notices table (append-only; one row per customer per

Contradicted By: NONE. Provider cost increases, FX movements, margin requirements, and
                 competitive pressures do not override this claim. WAOOAW absorbs mid-contract
                 cost increases and adjusts pricing at renewal. If a cost increase is so
                 severe that continuing the contract at the agreed price would make the
                 service unsustainable, the correct path is: give 30-day notice, offer the
                 customer a cancellation with pro-rata refund, or absorb and adjust at
                 renewal — never a unilateral mid-contract increase.

Reviewer Notes:  The 30-day notice period is the minimum. WAOOAW should target 45 days where
                 operationally feasible, giving customers time to evaluate and decide.

                 "Acknowledgment" does not require explicit confirmation — the customer
                 clicking "I agree" on every price change would be friction. Acknowledgment
                 is defined as: the customer received the WhatsApp notification (delivery
                 confirmed by Meta) AND did not cancel within the notice period. Silence
                 after delivery confirmation = acknowledgment. This is consistent with
                 standard subscription contract practice.

                 The auto-refill authorization is separately protected: if a customer
                 pre-authorized auto-refill at ₹99 per top-up and WAOOAW changes the
                 top-up price to ₹129, the new price requires its own 30-day notice and
                 re-authorization. The original ₹99 authorization is not valid for the
                 new price.
