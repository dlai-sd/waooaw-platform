## Blocker

WC-065 requires exact legal basis, recipient/redaction, payload-erasure, retention, remedy, and
grandfathering decisions before prospective customer-impacting behavior can be activated. The
approved Product, Data, Security, and Constitutional owner records each preserve a distinct legal
or legal/privacy owner for the exact legal conclusions.

The Institution Registry contains INST-001 through INST-014 and INST-CI-001, but no Legal
institution. `constitution/AGENT-ENTRY.md` contains a knowledge-routing row for a WAOOAW AI Agent
- Legal; that row is not an Institution charter, assigns no INST identifier, records no
`OPERATIONAL` status, and supplies no GEOM Decision Space. GEOM G-7 and Stage G-4 permit INST-013
to issue GO Authorizations only to registered OPERATIONAL Institutions.

INST-013 therefore cannot issue a legal-owner GOA, invent an Institution ID, let Product, Data,
Security, Constitutional, or Founder authority absorb the missing legal Decision Space, or mark
the legal input not applicable without an accountable legal verdict.

## Required Resolution

The Founder must select and constitutionally complete one valid path:

1. Charter and activate a Legal institution with a specific Decision Space, reviewer,
   constitutional obligations, and Institution Registry entry, then route the bounded legal
   contribution through GEOM; or
2. Amend an existing OPERATIONAL institution's charter to own the exact legal/privacy decisions,
   with independent constitutional review and Founder ratification before routing; or
3. Amend WC-065 through its accountable Product, Business, Data, Security, Constitutional, and
   architecture owners to remove the affected prospective-change capability and acceptance
   obligations, then repeat dependency-impact, integrated review, and Constitutional readiness.

An AGENT-ENTRY routing label, model answer, Founder preference, implementation default, or
fail-closed runtime behavior does not resolve the missing Decision Space.
